For financial advisers and estate or elder-law attorneys

Video for a practice built on trust.

People pick an adviser or an attorney they believe. Before the first call, they look for you. What they find is a name, a bio and a few lines on a website.

Video lets them hear you answer a question the way you answer it in your office. A short clip about what to gather before a meeting, or what a power of attorney does, tells a stranger how you explain things.

We make that part easy. You record on your phone. We cut it, write the captions and keep your accounts moving for ninety days. Nothing goes out until your approver has signed it.

Book your free conversation Pick a 45-minute slot on Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday, between 10 am and 1 pm Eastern.

How it works

  1. 1We look first. A free conversation about your practice, who you want to reach and who signs off on your posts.
  2. 2You record, your approver signs. You record short answers to the questions clients ask you. We turn them into posts. Your compliance approver signs each week’s batch before anything is scheduled.
  3. 3We run it with you. Ninety days of steady posting, two 30-minute calls a week in month one, then one call a week of 60 minutes at most, and the numbers each month in plain words.

What you get

  • •Short videos from your answers. In your first week you record ten short videos on your phone. Three go out as soon as your approver signs them.
  • •Your accounts, produced for ninety days. Captions and posting, with one goal for each month.
  • •A website, built or fixed. Your name, your firm’s name, your office address and phone, and one clear next step.
  • •A newsletter, started. The list, the first issue and the habit. A draft waits for your approval every time.
  • •Your week, sorted. We set up tools that take the dull parts off your week. Follow-ups go out on time, bookings land on your calendar, posts go out on schedule. You decide which ones you keep.
  • •Your events, filmed. If you hold a workshop or speak at one, we film it, under the rules below.
  • •A campaign in month three. Built around the goal we set together in the first conversation. At day 75 we go through the numbers with you.

How we work inside your compliance rules

Your compliance officer or bar counsel decides what meets your rules. We do not give legal or compliance advice. What we commit to:

  1. 1Approval first. Nothing is scheduled until your compliance approver has signed the week’s batch.
  2. 2Your records. Each month you get a dated archive of every post, caption and approval, for your own records.
  3. 3No claims. Your posts make no claims about results, returns, dollar amounts or comparisons. A client speaks on camera only with the disclosure your rules require, inside the video, and with your approver’s signature.
  4. 4Workshops. At a workshop the camera is on you, and the room is filmed from behind. Anyone who can be recognised signs a release first. Nobody who attends is approached for business afterwards.
  5. 5Your name on it. Your name, your firm’s name and your office address and phone are on every piece.

We never put a child on camera or in a caption, named or tagged.

What it costs

The 90-Day Relaunch is $2,500, paid in three monthly payments. You pay $1,000 to start, then $750 at the start of month two and $750 at the start of month three. Each invoice comes by email and is due in seven days. You can cancel at the end of any month; what you have paid covers the months you got.

Want to try it first? One Relaunch month is $1,000, then $500 a month, month to month.

After the ninety days, $500 a month on contract, for as long as we are worth keeping. We keep your socials active and take the boring parts. We turn your videos and voice notes into your newsletter, and you approve the final draft. We maintain your website and fill the gaps.

Ad spend, printing and shipping are yours and are not included unless we agree otherwise in writing. Travel and meals for filming away from home are at cost, agreed with you before the event.

We do not promise sales, income or followers. We promise to work as hard as we can, and to be as intentional as possible, toward the results you want. Every month you see what we posted and what happened after.

To start, book a free conversation. Or tell us about yourself first, and if we are a fit we write back with times to talk.
