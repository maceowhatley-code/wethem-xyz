From Crypto with Kamal, by Kamal Hubbard

Maceo Whatley of We Them Media

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 13 October 2023 · 17:50

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Kamal Hubbard sits down with Maceo Whatley at the 6th Annual Black Blockchain Summit, on the Howard University campus in Washington, DC, where Kamal is working with ICP Hub North America. It is Maceo’s first time at the summit, and he calls it “great to be in the presence of beautiful Black brilliance.”

Maceo traces his way in: seeing Bitcoin in the movie Dope, a college roommate trading NFTs, and the ENS airdrop that made him decide “I’m about to become a crypto bro.” He talks about bringing historically marginalized voices into Web3, starting his podcast to network with other artists, and music NFTs, which he says let artists “own your relationship to your patrons and your consumer and your fans.” He wants “meaningful allyship instead of performative allyship,” and his advice to a musician starting out is to talk to people already doing it. Kamal says this is why he wrote his book, DeFi for the Diaspora.
