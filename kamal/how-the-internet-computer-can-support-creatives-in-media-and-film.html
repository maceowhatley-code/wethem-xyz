How the Internet Computer can support creatives in media and film

Kamal Hubbard · posted to Crypto with Kamal on 23 May 2024 · 12:52

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Aaron, who works in production for the Internet Computer community, talks with Kamal Hubbard about using the network for film and music. He says it is “pretty much the only platform that can actually host the creative assets inside of it,” where a film or a song can literally live inside a smart contract, unlike platforms that only point to files stored elsewhere.

Aaron describes losing money as a filmmaker when a distributor went out of business, and his own project since day one, the Stoken Protocol, meant to let credits and royalties on something like a film “flow through in a smart contract way.” He imagines a world where “everyone who gets a piece of that film gets paid instantly every time someone rents it,” instead of filmmakers waiting six months to get paid.
