TrapDeFi: from the block to the blockchain and ICP (Part 1)

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 23 February 2024 · 18:04

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Kamal Hubbard talks with TrapDeFi, who describes himself: “I’m a regular street dude, man.” He explains his name: “I got the name Trap and then I came to DeFi.” He has been in crypto since 2018, when he put around $16,000 into Bitcoin while in debt, and later applied the same instincts, buying low and selling high, to DeFi.

TrapDeFi describes becoming an ICP holder after his Telegram account was suspended in October 2023 and a friend pointed him to OpenChat, built on the Internet Computer. He bought in around $2.86 a token, with the price since climbing to around $13, and gives his rule for holding: “It’s not about timing the market. It’s about time in the market.”
