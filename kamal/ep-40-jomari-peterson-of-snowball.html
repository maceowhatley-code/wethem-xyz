From Crypto with Kamal, by Kamal Hubbard

Jomari Peterson of Snowball

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 30 September 2023 · 6:29

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Jomari Peterson talks with Kamal Hubbard at the Black Blockchain Summit about Snowball, a DAO he built on Avalanche that deployed “$300 million in contracts,” and PhiHub, the project he started afterward to fix what he calls “a fragmentation issue”: the way using Web3 assets means checking a different site, Twitter and Discord just to make one decision.

Jomari also describes his work with artists making algorithmic art, both AI-generated and code-based generative art. For AI art, he says a creator can “put your training data that you use, hash it, and have that on-chain,” giving a public record of what was used to make a piece. For generative art, the collector’s own randomness at the moment of minting becomes part of how the piece looks.
