From Crypto with Kamal, by Kamal Hubbard

Carlos Ivan Merino of the NJ Digital Assets Working Group

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 19 October 2023 · 23:21

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Kamal Hubbard talks with Carlos Ivan Merino, a member of the New Jersey Digital Assets Working Group. Merino describes moving from TV and radio into local government, then writing a 2018 paper with interns that became a blockchain bill the governor signed in 2019. He first heard of the Internet Computer in 2021 and brushed it off, then two years later, watching a tutorial video, he says: “I just went down the rabbit hole.”

He calls New Jersey’s public sector “aware of blockchain, but they don’t utilize it,” and says its task force has sat dormant since COVID. What drew him to the Internet Computer was a site on-chain with no gas fees. He explains ChainKey Bitcoin, which lets someone deposit Bitcoin and receive a Bitcoin “twin” that moves at ICP speed, and points to Lugano, Switzerland, where residents pay for city services through an app built on the network.
