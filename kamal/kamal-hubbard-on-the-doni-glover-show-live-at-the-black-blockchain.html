Kamal Hubbard on the Doni Glover Show, live at the Black Blockchain Summit

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 23 September 2023 · 1:12:56

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

This is an episode of the Doni Glover Show, hosted by Donnie Glover of BlackUSA.News and BeMoreNews.com, recorded live during the 6th Annual Black Blockchain Summit at Howard University. Donnie calls it “a special edition” covering blockchain and crypto, admitting up front, “I don’t know too much about crypto,” before bringing on Kamal Hubbard, there representing ICP Hub North America.

The show also brings on attorney Eric Guthrie, author of Blockchain or Die, who describes his book as bringing “all the technology and all the learnings about cryptocurrencies and blockchains in plain, easy-to-speak language,” and later Dr. Taborn of STEM City USA. Guthrie closes with his standing advice to anyone getting into crypto: “Never invest in anything you do not understand.”

DeFi for the Diaspora

The history of money, where it has left Black communities around the world, and how decentralized finance could be used to change that.
