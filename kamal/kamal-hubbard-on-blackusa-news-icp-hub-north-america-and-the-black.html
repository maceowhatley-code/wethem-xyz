From Crypto with Kamal, by Kamal Hubbard

Kamal Hubbard on BlackUSA.News: ICP Hub North America and the Black Blockchain Summit

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 12 September 2023 · 31:25

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Kamal Hubbard joins Donnie Glover on BlackUSA.News ahead of the 6th Annual Black Blockchain Summit at Howard University, where Hubbard will represent ICP Hub North America. He walks through basic vocabulary for viewers, explaining “blockchain is essentially a type of ledger,” and describing the 12 seed words that secure a crypto wallet. Asked how he got into crypto, he traces it to studying the subprime mortgage crisis and shareholder lawsuits against banks: “banks created this crisis.”

He says “I took 2 years of independent study just to understand what Bitcoin was and how it worked” before buying in. He cites a Pew Research study on higher crypto adoption among Black Americans, and describes ICP’s integration with the Bitcoin network for cheaper, faster transactions, plus grants it offers Black developers building on it. He also names his certifications in fraud examination, cybersecurity and decentralized finance, and his book, DeFi for the Diaspora.

Kamal's book

DeFi for the Diaspora

The history of money, where it has left Black communities around the world, and how decentralized finance could be used to change that.
