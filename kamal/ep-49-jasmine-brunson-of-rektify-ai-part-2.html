From Crypto with Kamal, by Kamal Hubbard

Jasmine Brunson of Rektify.AI Part 2

Kamal Hubbard · posted to Crypto with Kamal on 21 October 2023 · 26:09

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Jasmine Brunson of Rektify AI continues her conversation with Kamal Hubbard, picking up on venture capital funding for Black women before turning to the Internet Computer. She has held ICP and done a hackathon with the network, and says “it’s going to be hard to kind of like fend against ETH.” She also walks through which language a beginner should learn first, recommending JavaScript or Python before smart contract languages like Solidity or Cairo.

Brunson also revisits Bitcoin maximalism, recalling “It was so much toxic Bitcoin maximalism at the Bitcoin 2021 conference, to the point where I even tuned out,” and argues for bringing Bitcoin to communities left out of Western finance rather than shaming people for not buying it. She closes by pointing listeners to Rektify AI’s GitHub, Twitter and newsletter, and explains her own handle, Bubbly Orca, taken from Elizabeth Warren calling crypto coders “shadowy super coders.”

Kamal's book

DeFi for the Diaspora

The history of money, where it has left Black communities around the world, and how decentralized finance could be used to change that.
