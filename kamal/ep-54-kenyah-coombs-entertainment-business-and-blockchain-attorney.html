Kenyah Coombs: entertainment, business and blockchain attorney

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 9 February 2024 · 31:20

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Kenyah Coombs, an Ontario attorney who says “my practice intersects between business, entertainment, and tech,” talks with Kamal Hubbard about the Canadian trucker convoy that protested vaccine mandates in 2022. Some truckers were funded through Bitcoin once bank transfers were frozen under the government’s Emergency Act, a workaround Coombs says was possible because the rules “weren’t designed to deal with Bitcoin.” She draws a parallel to Bitcoin’s role in funding the January 6th events in the United States.

Coombs also talks about her work as legal counsel for NetraScale, which uses AI against cybersecurity threats, including crypto investment scams. She walks through how one works: a victim sends money and gets “what looks like a login and password to view this platform and watch my investment grow,” then is asked for more money before any withdrawal, which never comes. She says she has seen close to $3 million in client losses from scams like it.
