Adam McCoy on his role as community relations at The Swap

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 20 May 2024 · 7:07

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Kamal Hubbard talks with Adam McCoy, community relations manager at The Swap, broadcasting from Milwaukee during ICPCC 2024. Adam describes the role as being “more of a liaison to people,” making sure a project understands what its community wants instead of focusing only on “the tech and the money.” He says community gets sidelined too often in Web3, and “that always frustrated me.”

Adam traces his path into Web3 through his brother, Daniel McCoy, The Swap’s chief technical officer. He describes himself as a believer in “chain agnosticism,” and spends time on conference floors and Twitter Spaces bringing back what other ecosystems are building. His view on tribalism between chains: “there doesn’t have to be this tribalism in Web3 where we’re all against each other.”

And I saw that Kyle and TheSwap had great potential where You know, they’re very involved with the Internet Computer ecosystem and they seem to be one of the few groups of people who was actually trying to make a charge with the community saying, hey, we’re going to keep you updated.
