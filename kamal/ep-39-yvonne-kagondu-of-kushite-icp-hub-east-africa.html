From Crypto with Kamal, by Kamal Hubbard

Yvonne Kagondu of Kushite ICP Hub East Africa

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 22 September 2023 · 24:17

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Yvonne Kagondu, co-founder of Kushite ICP Hub East Africa, talks with Kamal Hubbard at the 6th Annual Black Blockchain Summit. She studied financial economics in Nairobi, then found blockchain after a software bootcamp: “it was like financial economics on steroids.” She found Kenya’s Bitcoin community in 2018, and years later ICP recruited her through her advocacy training Kenyan women in the technology: “I didn’t find ICP, but ICP found me.”

She explains why so much of Kenya’s crypto activity happens peer to peer: after the Central Bank of Kenya barred banks from touching cryptocurrency in 2015, young freelancers turned to Bitcoin because “PayPal is very discriminatory for Africans,” cashing out locally for 0 to 2% while buyers elsewhere pay a premium. Kushite ICP Hub East Africa has since run campus tours at about 10 Kenyan universities and events across East and West Africa.
