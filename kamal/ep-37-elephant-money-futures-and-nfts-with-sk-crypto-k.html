Elephant Money futures and NFTs with SK Crypto K

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 21 July 2023 · 32:10

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Kamal Hubbard’s second conversation with SK Crypto K about Elephant Money covers Futures, which SK describes as “a high-yield savings account where you will lock up your principal” and earn about half a percent a day in BUSD, with a $200 minimum to compound the payout back into the balance rather than claim it.

They also cover Elephant Money’s new NFTs: 10,000 available in the first round at 1 BNB each, currently earning about 66 percent APR from the project’s treasury, where “each subsequent round of 10,000 that come out, they double in price.” SK says of the token, “This is a long-term hold,” pointing to a chart where the treasury has outpaced the debt owed to Futures depositors many times over.
