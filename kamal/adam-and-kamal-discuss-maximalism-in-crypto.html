Adam and Kamal discuss maximalism in crypto

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 28 May 2024 · 8:58

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Adam McCoy and Kamal Hubbard talk about Bitcoin maximalism, prompted by the rise of Ordinals and BRC-20 tokens. One of them admits, “I am a maxi for ICP just because I’m so passionate about, you know, its proposition to an open internet,” while arguing that strict maximalism is “slightly irrational in a certain sense.” They agree Ordinals bring real community and volume to Bitcoin rather than just clutter its ledger.

They also discuss ChainKey Bitcoin, the Internet Computer’s way of transacting in Bitcoin without its usual delays. They credit a friend, Bob Bodily of Tonic and Bionic, for explaining ICP as “a sidechain, it being used as another chain to the side to be able to facilitate everything,” taking on the burden of Bitcoin’s slower network. One of them says the technology “helps for people coming into the ecosystem” and “helps for adoption.”
