FTX conspiracy theories with Bryce Weiner, Part 2

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 26 November 2022 · 22:58

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Part two of Kamal Hubbard’s conversation with Bryce Weiner about the conspiracy theories around FTX’s collapse. They cover the political donations Sam Bankman-Fried made to both parties; Weiner argues the sums were “a drop in an ocean of money” next to total campaign spending, making the donations a smaller story than online conspiracy theories claim.

Weiner explains Alameda Research, FTX’s trading arm, as “the fuel supply system” for the exchange, and describes how customer funds were commingled between the two companies until there was nothing left to give back. His fix is to make big crypto firms report their lending the way banks do, so everyone can see how much debt is really out there. The episode ends on the line it opened with: cryptocurrency “is utterly irrelevant to the crimes that were committed.”
