From Crypto with Kamal, by Kamal Hubbard

Jasmine Brunson of Rektify.AI

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 16 October 2023 · 26:45

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Jasmine Brunson, CTO, CEO and co-founder of Rektify AI, introduces herself to Kamal Hubbard at the 6th Annual Black Blockchain Summit. A former data scientist at NASA and Maxar, she watched the Poly Network and Ronin Network hacks happen in real time and pivoted into Web3 security, saying it was “where it’s at when it comes to job opportunities.” She defines a smart contract as “cryptographic code that enforces instructions.”

She left Maxar in February 2022 to build what became Rektify AI at ETH Denver, picking up a grant for Black women founders and a win at the Black Bitcoin Billionaires pitch competition. Rektify AI now audits smart contracts by hand, hunting bugs like reentrancy, with automated tools covering the rest. Reflecting on being a Black woman in Web3 since attending the Black Blockchain Summit in 2019, she says “no one can stop us and no one can touch us.”
