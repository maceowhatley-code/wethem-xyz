From Crypto with Kamal, by Kamal Hubbard

RJ McDowell of Repar Global Coin

Kamal Hubbard · posted to Crypto with Kamal on 13 May 2023 · 32:26

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

RJ McDowell, founder of the Repar Global Coin project, traces its start to a pandemic-era social media exchange with a fashion influencer, whom he asked, “can I ask you a question without offending you?” about her never featuring Black-owned brands. That conversation grew into an idea for an Amazon-style marketplace for Black-owned brands with its own cryptocurrency rebates, first discussed around 2020.

He explains the name: “we’re abbreviating the name Repar for reparations,” tied to 1865, the year slavery was abolished. The token’s supply is 18,650,000,000, with 1,865 tokens airdropped to the first 50,000 people each Juneteenth and December 18th. McDowell says the project focuses on “the wealth gap, the technology gap, and recidivism,” and runs on the Stellar network rather than Ethereum, to keep transaction fees low.
