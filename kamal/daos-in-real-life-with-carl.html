DAOs in real life with Carl

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 21 May 2024 · 11:28

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Carl, of Craft the Future, talks with Kamal Hubbard about working at a DAO, Aragon Network DAO, which he calls falling into by accident at the start of his time in Web3: “I always say that I fell into that.” He credits a colleague named Renee for showing him that most explainers were, in his words, “like the old white man with the, with the Google Slide PowerPoint,” not built to make anyone want to learn.

Carl defines a DAO simply: “it requires all of us in this room to be in the consensus together to know what we want, our objectives, and be able to work towards that.” He argues a DAO still needs real accountability, using the example of picking a marketing director: “did we all agree on Carl to be the marketing director?” His advice to anyone starting one: “you got to be prepared to fail.”
