From Crypto with Kamal, by Kamal Hubbard

Alexander Rees-Evans of Kaizen Finance

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 15 November 2023 · 18:47

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Alexander Rees-Evans, international sales director at Kaizen Finance, talks with Kamal Hubbard about his path into crypto, which started about 5 years earlier helping Idoneus, a Florida company that let people barter luxury assets like diamonds, cars and yachts for a token called IDLE. He brought the French royal family’s champagne house onto the platform: “So the royal family of France now accepts crypto.” From there he ran sales at a large exchange before a friend at Kaizen recruited him.

Kaizen Finance runs out of Kyiv and is opening a Dubai office, helping projects launch with vesting, staking and anti front-runner tools that offset “about 80% selling pressure.” For Ocean Money, which had about 10,700 Twitter followers, Kaizen’s presale tech raised about $320,000 in weeks. He closes: “check everything, verify everything, stay on top of everything, and you’ll be great.”
