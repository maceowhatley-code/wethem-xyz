From Crypto with Kamal, by Kamal Hubbard

Eric Guthrie, author of Blockchain or Die

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 13 October 2023 · 18:29

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Kamal Hubbard talks with Eric Guthrie, author of Blockchain or Die, at Howard University during the 6th Annual Black Blockchain Summit, where Hubbard represents ICP Hub North America. Guthrie explains the book’s title with a question: “Do you do anything now in life without the internet?” His case: companies that adopt blockchain will survive the way companies that adopted the internet did, and those that don’t will end up like Blockbuster or Radio Shack.

They cover the Corporate Transparency Act, requiring companies to file ownership details with the Treasury’s Financial Crimes Enforcement Network from January 1st, 2024, with penalties up to $10,000 or two years in jail for skipping it. Guthrie also leads the Government Blockchain Association, working to bring blockchain into services like tax payment and car registration. He closes with two rules for investing: “never invest in anything you do not understand,” and “never invest in anything you cannot afford to lose.”
