From Crypto with Kamal, by Kamal Hubbard

Tavonia Evans of GuapCoin

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 5 October 2023 · 15:11

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Tavonia Evans, founder of GuapCoin, talks with Kamal Hubbard about starting the project before she knew crypto existed: “We don’t really have a Black dollar,” she remembers thinking, while researching alternative community currencies. A friend introduced her to Bitcoin around 2009 without telling her it was money; once it clicked, she says, “we need our own crypto. We need our own blockchain. This solves so many problems for us.”

Evans describes launching a token on Ethereum in 2017 before building GuapCoin’s own chain later that year, and funding its growth through masternodes rather than outside investors, saying “I’ve learned the benefits of not having money in this industry.” She and Hubbard also discuss GuapCoin’s layer 2, launched in January, and her work with ICP Hub North America, including plans for on-chain identity tools and “.guap” NFT domain names.
