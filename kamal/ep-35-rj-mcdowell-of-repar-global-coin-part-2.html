From Crypto with Kamal, by Kamal Hubbard

RJ McDowell of REPAR Global Coin Part 2

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 20 May 2023 · 30:21

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

RJ McDowell of REPAR Global Coin returns to talk with Kamal Hubbard about building the project without an initial coin offering, which lawyers warned was illegal in the United States, so she funded it with grants, sweat equity and her retirement savings, and taught herself coding along the way. She also talks about being a Black woman in crypto spaces around Washington, DC: “I show up, I kid you not, I’m the only one in the room,” and once had someone ask who was really in charge.

Her advice to newcomers is to hold their own private key, “No keys, no crypto,” and to check whether a project has a real white paper and a founder people can actually find. She names other tokens she sees carrying real utility on banking payment rails, XRP, XLM, HBAR, XDC, Quant and Algorand, and notes REPAR itself runs by way of Stellar.
