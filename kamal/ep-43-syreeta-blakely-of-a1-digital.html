From Crypto with Kamal, by Kamal Hubbard

Syreeta Blakely of A1 Digital

Kamal Hubbard · posted to Crypto with Kamal on 7 October 2023 · 13:19

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Kamal Hubbard talks with Syreeta Blakely of A1 Digital, who wrote a flip-book with her partner Cleophis Harbison called “The ABCs of Cryptocurrencies for Newbies,” paired on the other side with a crypto compilation. Syreeta, a former educator, says they wrote it because “People need to know some basic foundation stuff about like crypto,” after making expensive mistakes of their own starting in 2017.

Syreeta also describes discovering Elephant Money at a conference, which she calls “the first global decentralized community bank,” and says what drew her in was hearing its founder say, “I want to make sure that food is on everybody’s table,” rather than run a pump and dump project. Her advice for anyone getting into crypto, repeated twice: “do your homework, right? Do your homework.”

The history of money, where it has left Black communities around the world, and how decentralized finance could be used to change that.
