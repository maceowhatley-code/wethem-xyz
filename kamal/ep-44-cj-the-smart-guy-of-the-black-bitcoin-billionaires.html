From Crypto with Kamal, by Kamal Hubbard

CJ the Smart Guy of the Black Bitcoin Billionaires

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 11 October 2023 · 14:00

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

CJ, known online as CJ the Smart Guy, talks with Kamal Hubbard at the Black Blockchain Summit about his path from working in accounting for a Warren Buffett-owned railroad to becoming, in his words, “the local resident CPA of the Black Bitcoin billionaires.” He says people in crypto are less intimidated by taxes than they are rebellious, and he tries to correct that: “a lot of people perish because of a lack of knowledge.”

CJ’s main advice is to “double-check your tax returns from 2019 to 2022” to make sure the digital-asset question is answered honestly, since answering no while holding crypto is “considered fraud.” He also explains borrowing against an appreciated asset instead of selling it, and repeats the community’s own rule about self-custody: “no keys, no cheese.”
