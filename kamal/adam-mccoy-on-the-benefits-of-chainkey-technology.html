Adam McCoy on the benefits of ChainKey technology

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 29 May 2024 · 7:00

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Adam McCoy talks with Kamal Hubbard about ChainKey technology, the Internet Computer’s way of handling assets like Bitcoin without wrapping them in a token. He says “wrapping a token is clunky” and “can take a little bit longer” compared with ChainKey, while admitting the deep technical side is “my brother’s area,” a nod to his brother Daniel.

Adam highlights the network’s “reverse gas fee model where, you know, the developers pay the cost in cycles,” so users pay nothing to use it, unlike networks where fees can wipe out a purchase: he recently paid “$350 to swap it to Ethereum” for a meme coin. He credits people building on the Internet Computer, including Bob Bodily, Easy Steve and Jordan Lass, alongside his brother Daniel.
