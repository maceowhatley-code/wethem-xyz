What I got wrong about Elephant Money

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 21 November 2023 · 8:33

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Kamal Hubbard revisits Elephant Money, a BNB-based DeFi project, after losing the audio from an interview with its founder, known as Bank Teller, at the Black Blockchain Summit. He apologizes for the mistake and describes what he learned about Bank Teller anyway: that “he’s someone in DeFi who’s willing to show his face,” that he was generous with his time, and that he sponsored a free crypto book for conference attendees.

Kamal then reviews his own numbers. An NFT he minted in January never showed a loss, reaching a high of 452% profit this year and sitting near 300% at the time of recording. He explains Elephant Money Futures, which “earns 0.5% daily on cash” for depositors of $200 or more, and Elephant Money Unlimited, an NFT that lets holders exit at a higher BNB price in later rounds. He notes the project is “the number 2 holder of BNB in the BNB ecosystem.”
