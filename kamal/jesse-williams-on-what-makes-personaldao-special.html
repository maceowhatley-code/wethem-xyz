Jesse Williams on what makes PersonalDAO special

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 17 May 2024 · 17:02

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Jesse Williams, founder of PersonalDAO, joins Kamal Hubbard to explain what he built on the Internet Computer. He found the network at its Genesis launch event on 11 May 2021 and started buying ICP token as the price fell from about $700 to $2.82, saying he “rode and built” through that entire drop.

PersonalDAO is gated rather than permissionless, and each community gets its own copy rather than a shared platform: “it’s a turnkey DAO that’s meant to be deployed to the internet in multiple replicas, multiple instances,” with its own database and treasury. He compares it to franchises: “You can have 2 different McDonald’s. If one is hit by a meteor, the other one is completely intact.” The design is meant for lending among people who already know each other, rather than an institution.
