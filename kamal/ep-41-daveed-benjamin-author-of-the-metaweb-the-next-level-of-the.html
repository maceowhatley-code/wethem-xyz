From Crypto with Kamal, by Kamal Hubbard

Daveed Benjamin, author of The Metaweb: The Next Level of the Internet

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 3 October 2023 · 27:38

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Daveed Benjamin talks with Kamal Hubbard at the 6th Annual Black Blockchain Summit in Washington, DC, about his book, The MetaWeb: The Next Level of the Internet, and about Ordinals, inscriptions of up to 4 megabytes of data written onto individual satoshis of Bitcoin. He calls them digital artifacts rather than NFTs: unlike an Ethereum NFT that can point to a file stored elsewhere and go missing, an Ordinal’s information lives fully on the Bitcoin blockchain, and, he says, “That will never happen with an Ordinal.”

Benjamin also lays out the metaweb, which he calls “the entire space above the web page,” and the Overweb, a trust layer built on decentralized identity. His book publishes 9 October through Taylor and Francis, runs over 300 pages with about 40 illustrations, and comes with an NFT edition readers can annotate; more is at OrdWeb.io and metawebbook.com.
