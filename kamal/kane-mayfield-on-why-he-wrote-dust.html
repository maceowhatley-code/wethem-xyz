Kane Mayfield on why he wrote Dust

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 28 January 2024 · 5:03

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

Kamal Hubbard talks with rapper Kane Mayfield about an album he wrote in 2016 and only released years later. Kane says the writing began after he lost both parents: “I also lost both my parents, so I was not in the best place in my head.” He left Baltimore for Florida, then Mexico, where he met his wife, after his first album, The Return of Rap, “didn’t do great.”

Kane describes coming back to Baltimore to two sold-out one-man shows, “No openers, no feature, no bringing my friends up,” and finding his old graffiti tags painted over. That anger shaped the new record’s sound: “The music started to sound like how I talk.”
