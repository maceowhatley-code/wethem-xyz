How the blockchain can decrease fraud in the music industry

Kamal Hubbard, posted to Crypto with Kamal on 8 June 2017 · 12:09

This is Kamal’s video, shown here with his permission. Watch it on his channel, where the comments are.

A 2017 conversation about how blockchain could cut fraud in the music industry, centered on Kamal Hubbard’s account of getting to know Bryce and the ideas that came out of it. Kamal explains payola, the old practice where “music organizations or managers would pay radio stations to play songs.” The interviewer calls it “It’s just bribery, really,” and Kamal agrees.

They also discuss how blockchain could work for musicians beyond payola: a shared record of band names, where a group can point back and say “we staked our claim 2 years ago,” and songs stored with a digital signature so a writer’s claim to a piece of work is verifiable. Kamal argues the technology could let artists track how their music is used, and let administrators add value handling the technical side instead of being replaced by it.
