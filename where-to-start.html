Where to start.

97 pieces to watch first. People explaining things, without the heavy parts.

This is not a filter and nothing is hidden. Everything on this site is still where it was, and a piece missing from this page means only that nobody picked it, not that there is anything wrong with it. We are not calling these safe, or suitable for any particular age, because that is not a thing we can promise about a person talking about their own life.

What we can say is what these have in common. Somebody explains something in each one, the language is whatever it was on the street, and where a hard subject comes up it is framed rather than left raw. Every one was read start to finish, twice, by a machine rather than a person, before it went on this list. How that was done, and why it is weaker than a person reading, is at the foot of the page.

How these were picked.

A script counted strong language across all 137 candidates and looked for the shape of somebody explaining something. Each of those transcripts was then read in full, twice, under two different lenses, and marked for language, how heavy the material is, whether a hard thing is framed or left raw, and whether anybody actually teaches you anything. Only pieces both reads cleared are here. The two reads reached the same verdict on 124 of 136.

The honest caveat: those were machine reads, not people. Two of them can be wrong in the same way, which two people usually are not. So treat this as a reasonable place to begin and nothing more.

One rule changed on 13 September 2026. The strong-language count fell hardest on the American street pieces, Denver above all, and barely at all on the Nigerian, Kenyan and Ethiopian ones: a bias in the method against the way people talk on some of these streets, not a judgement of the pieces. So language on its own no longer keeps a piece off this page. Maceo’s words: if the content is meaningful, the language is trivial. What still counts is whether somebody explains something and how a hard subject is handled.

If a piece here does not belong, say so. In one of the videos? Message us on Instagram (@we_them_media) or X (@WeThemMediaHQ) and we will get back to you within two weeks. Every page on this site also carries a correction panel, headed Something wrong here?, and one of the things you can pick in it is I am in this and I want it changed or taken down. That goes for anybody in any piece, whether or not it is on this page.
