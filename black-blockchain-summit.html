Black Blockchain Summit.

Howard University, Washington, DC, 2024 and 2026

We Them Media spent the 2026 Black Blockchain Summit at Howard University asking students, a professor and speakers about money, Bitcoin, inflation and the dollar. The summit was co-founded by John Wainaina Karanja and Sinclair Skinner.

19 pieces are here, all from the summit in Washington, DC. Every one is public and has its own page with a full transcript.
