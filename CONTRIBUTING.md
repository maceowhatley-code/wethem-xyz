# Contributing

Thank you. This is a record of what people said, so the rules are about being careful.

1. **Names and terms are corrected. Speech is not.** Fix a misspelled name or a mis-heard term. Do not change what a person said.
2. **Check against the tape.** Every transcript paragraph on the site links to its second on YouTube. Say in your pull request
   which moment you checked.
3. **One change, one pull request,** with a plain description.
4. **Sign off your commits** (`git commit -s`). That is the Developer Certificate of Origin (see `DCO.md`): you wrote it, or have
   the right to contribute it, and it may be used under the licence.
5. **Do not edit generated pages.** Most pages are generated from the transcripts and facts. Change the source file and the page
   follows. If you are unsure, open an issue.
6. **No private information.** No emails, phone numbers or details of anyone who has not published them.
7. **Be kind.** See `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.

Every pull request is reviewed by a person before it is merged. First-time contributors' checks need approval to run.
