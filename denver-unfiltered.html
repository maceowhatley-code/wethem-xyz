Denver Unfiltered.

Denver, Colorado, 2024 and 2026

People in Denver and nearby talking about housing, music, grief, politics, crypto and what made them smile. Filmed on the street, in shops, at First Friday and at conferences, mostly in spring 2026.

87 pieces are here: 68 from Denver and around Colorado, and 19 from the Channel 5 Carnival day. Every one is public and has its own page. 81 have a full transcript; the other 6 are music or a room full of people, and their pages say so.
