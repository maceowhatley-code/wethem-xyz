The chip comes home, and a whole new New York.

Shan Wallace, New York, 8 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

To quote this piece: Shan Wallace, “The chip comes home, and a whole new New York,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-06, https://wethem.xyz/watch/the-curse-is-broken-street-reactions-new-york. Each transcript line links to its second.

What does it mean to New York? Asked on a street on the night the Knicks won the championship, the first voice says: “We brought the chip home.” What it means for the city: “It means everything.”

The slogan has been updated in the street from Knicks in 4 to Knicks in 5. “That means that the curse is broken.” “It’s a whole new New York.” And: “We needed this. The city needed this. The state needed this. I think the world needed this.”

A man who just arrived from North Carolina says: “I mean, our lives are gonna change.” Then: “I hope.” The person filming, from Philadelphia, says: “I feel like it’s a morale boost.” His case for the night: “New York needed somebody to show them possible’s possible.”

Shan Wallace, a visual artist from Baltimore who has lived in the city for several years, says: “I love New York, and I’m happy to be here.” Seven minutes in New York, June 2026.
