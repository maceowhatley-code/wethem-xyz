Bam Bam’s first impressions of the Mile High City.

Bam Bam, Reported by Maceo, Denver, 8 min, filmed 3 March 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Answers What would you say to someone under ten?, see how other cities answered

Why did a Seattle raver move to Denver, and what does the scene here not have? Bam Bam says he stays for the people, and for the underground: phoneless warehouse parties, “out in the middle of nowhere, like under a bridge, in the sewers,” until five or six in the morning.

What he wants is hard style: “It’s more of a European thing. Netherlands, Berlin, Amsterdam.” Denver is dubstep and bass, and he finds the screechy end of it grating. He makes beats himself and names Griselda, Wu-Tang, MF DOOM and Westside Gunn.

Seattle against Denver: “Everyone in Seattle, the vibe is just like, stay away from me, I don’t want to talk to you. But Denver, most people are just chill.” He gives Washington the win on moss and big trees.

His message for anybody under eight: “only go to college if you really need to. If you don’t need a degree to do what you want to do, then don’t do it. Just do what you want.” Language is unfiltered; the transcript is bleeped where the captions were.
