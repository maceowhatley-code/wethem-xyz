ETHConf: blockchain en más de 40 universidades argentinas.

New York, 4 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

An Argentine at ETHConf in New York is working to get more people across Latin America, governments and schools above all, to learn and understand blockchain. In Spanish. When DevConnect was held in Argentina, it opened the door: “pudimos lograr que más de 40 universidades se acerquen y enseñen blockchain desde las aulas.”

Argentina took to crypto first, he says, because of dollars. People working for companies abroad could not get paid in them, so “Los trabajadores cross-border hicieron una migración natural a cripto para poder traer sus remesas”. Real use and education followed.

His message for builders keeps the technology in its place: “que piensen a la tecnología como un medio para poder resolver un problema real del mundo real.” What ETH is: “creo que es la gran computadora pública sobre la cual podemos correr y programar el mundo que viene.” And what comes next brings in AI, a world where we have to think “no solamente en la capa social y personas, sino también en la capa de agentes”.

Filmed in June 2026. The transcript below is his own words in Spanish rather than a translation.
