The group photo, and the real conversations that started after it.

Reported by Maceo, Philadelphia, 18 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Is Bitcoin just Wall Street now? Asked at the Bitcoin Pizza Day party in Philadelphia, Jake says it is: “That’s debatable. Bitcoin is just part of Wall Street at this point.” If everything collapses, he says, it gets wrecked exactly as much as the thing it was supposed to be an alternative to.

He first heard of Bitcoin in the Silk Road days: “This is for drugs and terrorists. This is not going to be a valuable thing anyone’s going to care about beyond that.” Bitcoin or pizza boxes? “Bitcoin has plateaued. The pizza boxes are nothing right now. We’re at the bottom.”

The man who keeps the global party running says: “I am just facilitating it. I’m out there changing the tyres on it. When somebody has a dispute, I am talking to them.” His background is a web series in South Philly: “If you want to learn how to organise people to do something, go make a film.”

There is also a trivia giveaway nobody can win and the group photo. Philadelphia, May 2026.
