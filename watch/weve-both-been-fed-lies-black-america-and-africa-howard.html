We've both been fed lies: Black America and Africa.

Washington, DC, 9 min, filmed 26 September 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

To quote this piece: “We've both been fed lies: Black America and Africa,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-09-26, https://wethem.xyz/watch/weve-both-been-fed-lies-black-america-and-africa-howard. Each transcript line links to its second.

On Day 2 of the Black Blockchain Summit 2026, CJ Kelly, CJ the Smart Guy, sits down with Maceo a second time. He talks about his book, Bitcoin in Black America 2, and its directory of “over 150 people” in the space, written because “if you fall behind, essentially you can become a slave to the technology.” Then the diaspora: Africans were told Americans are all rich, Americans were told Africans are all poor, and when he went he found “poor people in Africa eat better than middle-class people here in America.” His conclusion: “we’ve both been fed lies,” and “once you eat together, like, a lot of that’s gonna like disappear.” He hires in Kenya now and talks about Black wealth before integration, Greenwood in Tulsa, and a coup in one of the Carolinas he asks us to check: “Yeah, fact-check it please. I might be off on the years”. A note on the facts: the coup is Wilmington, North Carolina, November 1898, when white supremacists attacked Black neighborhoods and overthrew the local government, and North Carolina sent no Black Representative to Congress from 1901 until 1992 (US House, History, Art & Archives); in Tulsa in 1921 about 1,256 Greenwood homes were destroyed (Oklahoma Commission, 2001); a January 2025 executive order bars US agencies from creating a central bank digital currency (White House). And Maceo’s figure for our supporters at the end is high: on the day, We Them Media had 7 monthly supporters giving $128 a month in all (wethem.xyz/supporter).
