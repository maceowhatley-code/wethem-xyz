ETHConf: DeFi is confusing, even for developers.

New York, 4 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

To quote this piece: “ETHConf: DeFi is confusing, even for developers,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-06, https://wethem.xyz/watch/defi-is-confusing-even-for-developers-ethconf-nyc. Each transcript line links to its second.

At ETHConf in Manhattan, June 2026, Jordan Greenberg of Weehawken, New Jersey, a Web2 developer whose career “took in a different path than Web3,” is back to catch up: “Really just to catch up on what’s happening in the space.” Maceo has heard he is also the Valley Game high scorer: “you won the Valley Game high score now, that’s what I’ve learned.” Jordan: “I’ve got 6 minutes left, but yes,” and he liked the machine’s whole idea, “onboarded with a crypto wallet, like just by scanning a QR code and playing in person.” On DeFi, he is honest about the barrier: “the learning curve and like the barrier to entry is so big. Like, I’m a technical person and Even for me, it’s confusing and hard to keep up with.” What he wants instead is “some of these agentic investment bots or like yield farming bots where it kind of abstracts some of that complexity away from the user.” His message for builders: “Keep doing what you’re doing,” and on AI and Web3, “I think they’re going to be at the forefront together.”
