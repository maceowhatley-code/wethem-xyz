Two creators are in line for Kai Cenat’s Streamer University auditions in New York. One of them was making videos and could not find anyone to stay behind the camera. The other was the last player left on a basketball court and said he would. They have posted every day since, on every platform, and the Instagram went from around 60,000 to over 100,000, with 50,000 of that in two months. The cameraman makes underground music in a studio in his room. The clip ends with Jalen IRL, who wants Streamer University to see Guyana, a country he says people are sleeping on.
