Kafani asks Lagos four questions about crypto and community.

Reported by Kafani, Lagos, 52 min

To quote this piece: “Kafani asks Lagos four questions about crypto and community,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/kafani-asks-lagos-four-questions-about-crypto-and-community. Each transcript line links to its second.

Kafani files her Bitcoin Pizza Day 2026 street series from Lagos, Nigeria, asking attendees four questions to map how everyday Lagosians think about crypto, community, and the 16-year-old transaction that put Bitcoin on the map.

Kafani’s framework: “I’ll be asking people questions about what is Bitcoin Pizza Day. If they had 10,000 Bitcoin today, what would they do? What is their favorite pizza? And if they could ask anyone from around the world a question, what question would they ask?”

She opens with the history: “Bitcoin Pizza Day is a famous moment in crypto history. It marks the first widely recognized real-world purchase made with Bitcoin. Two pizzas from Papa John’s. On May 22, 2010, a programmer named Laszlo Hanyecz paid 10,000 Bitcoin for two pizzas. At that time, Bitcoin was still experimental.”

On community, one attendee: “Community is really important to me. It’s people that make you feel seen, people that make you feel safe, people that share the same interest, people that inspire you. Everybody deserves to be a part of a community.” On the community they want to see: “A community for people who do not follow societal standards. Open-minded, free. People that can express themselves well. And I want that community to be accepted too.”

On the gatekeeping myth, an attendee says “I’m not rich enough to be a crypto. Crypto broke.” Kafani pushes back: “I joined the crypto community with zero to my name. It’s not only about trading.”

And the question one participant would put to the world: “If I’m to ask a question, can we ask for money? I’ll be asking the world for money. Just give me the money. Or give me information. I think that’s better.”

Kafani is the network’s Content Producer and AI Lead, based in Lagos. She runs her own Kafani Tries educational series, produces field segments like this one, and is a SheFi Season 16 alumna. She now runs One Question.
