A content coin, and making governance a game.

Swarthyhatter, Denver, 8 min, filmed 10 March 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

To quote this piece: Swarthyhatter, “A content coin, and making governance a game,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-03-10, https://wethem.xyz/watch/building-a-dao-and-making-governance-a-game-denver. Each transcript line links to its second.

How should a small media project govern itself? On a working call, Swarthyhatter lays out a model box by box while the We Them Media side pushes back. He starts with the standard version: “In the legacy nouns model, you have your DAO and then you have a member and they send a transaction to the DAO that creates a proposal.” Then a vote. “Proposal plus vote equals outcome. And then consensus stops there.” His answer: “That’s not our process.”

In his version the builder DAO is “your multisig, it’s your community treasury, it’s where you do all your consensus,” for top level questions only. Underneath, a content proposal that passes deploys “a content coin”, with the content on IPFS and the hash on chain. Holders swap their community token into the coin, and the swap is the vote: “by swapping it into this content coin, I’ve decided, I’ve signaled that I want this decision to be made.”
