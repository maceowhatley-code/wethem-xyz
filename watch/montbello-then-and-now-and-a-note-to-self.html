Walking the East Side: street footage from Denver.

Lil J Money · Reported by Maceo · Denver · 6 min · filmed 8 March 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

How did Denver change in your lifetime? A 39-year-old who grew up in Montbello, who goes by Lil J Money, answers on a walk along Colfax. “Denver changed a lot.” On his neighbourhood: “Montbello changed a lot from when I was growing up, gang banging and all that. But now it changed a lot. No more gang banging in Montbello.” Is that better? “I don’t like it no more. Got white people out there, and rich white folks out there.”

He goes for a cigarette and does not come back. The camera stays on, and Maceo, alone on International Women’s Day, talks to it about why any of this is being filmed: while he is in Denver he wants to keep doing it. Denver, March 2026.
