Luis, who climbs.

To quote this piece: Luis, “Luis, who climbs,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-03-12, https://wethem.xyz/watch/climbing-gyms-and-meeting-people-in-denver. Each transcript line links to its second.

A Denver local explains why a climbing gym is where people in their twenties actually meet each other, and surprises a visitor from Pittsburgh with what else is on offer there.

Luis grew up in Denver and started climbing as a kid, “just something we would do on the weekends.” What brought him back is not the sport. “It’s a great way to get outdoors in the city. It’s a great way to meet people. Mid-twenties, everyone’s down to climb, have a beer after, kind of thing.”

The beer is the part that stops the interview. The person filming is from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania: “We do not have alcohol served at climbing gyms in Pittsburgh.” There is also, it turns out, a trivia night going on.

Luis makes the case without overselling it: “Good culture, great people, everyone’s friendly. Super inclusive sport. It’s the spot to be, for sure.”

The conversation then turns into a bounty question about whether the internet spreads information or misinformation, which is how most of these pieces were filed: one question, put to whoever was standing there, for a weekly prize.

Filmed by Tim Palka, one of two pieces he shot for this archive.
