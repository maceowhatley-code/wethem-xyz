How a roommate on a whitelist led to DAOs.

Reported by Maceo, Philadelphia, 3 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

To quote this piece: “How a roommate on a whitelist led to DAOs,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/how-i-got-into-crypto-philadelphia. Each transcript line links to its second.

What got you into crypto and community? Maceo answers the question he usually asks other people, to camera in Philadelphia, on day one of a week leading up to the Global Pizza Party: “This whole week we’re going to be talking about crypto and community.”

It started in college in 2021 with a roommate: “My college roommate was making money off some different DeFi plays. He was involved in some NFTs. He was on the Doodles whitelist.” Maceo says: “I’m like, what the fuck is this?” So he went and read.

The first community he joined was SharkDAO: “people coming together to fundraise, crowdfund, to buy like larger NFT projects, one particularly called Nouns.” What stuck was “collective funding and cooperative action.” His definition of a DAO: an innovation on cooperatives that uses blockchain for agreements and governance.
