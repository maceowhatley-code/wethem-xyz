What is unschooling? Raised half in the US, half in Costa Rica.

Reported by Maceo · Denver · 7 min · filmed 19 March 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week →

What is unschooling? A man raised half in the United States and half in Costa Rica says it still has Khan Academy and Duolingo sessions. What it removes is the curriculum: “Generally, unschooling is having your kids learn through life experience, which is what my mom did raising us. Instead of having us take a science class or history class, she would take us out to the museum, to the zoo, to actually see things.”

On growing up between two countries: “We grew up learning Spanish, we grew up socialising, we grew up seeing the world and learning about different cultures.”

His one year in a school system was sixth grade in Costa Rica: four classes a day, an hour each, with a half hour between. “So you actually have time to socialise and hang out with your friends and chill. And then when it comes time to be in class, you want to be, because you’ve had your time.” The conversation ends with four people comparing their Spanish.
