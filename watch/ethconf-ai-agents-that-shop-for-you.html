ETHConf: AI agents that shop for you.

Saif, New York, 5 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

To quote this piece: Saif, “ETHConf: AI agents that shop for you,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-06, https://wethem.xyz/watch/ethconf-ai-agents-that-shop-for-you. Each transcript line links to its second.

A builder from Michigan at ETHConf in New York describes his work in one line: “Right now I’ve been working on really trying to bring every merchant in front of AI.” Shopping went from stores to e-commerce, he says, and is going to agents that buy on your behalf. “We’re building that infrastructure so agents can know where to shop, what to shop, and what’s the price.”

What an agent is: “an AI agent is something that can execute any workflow on its own without actually needing human input at any stage.” Or, shorter, “you can think of it like bots on steroids”. For people outside tech, he sees the start of an adoption curve, the way the internet became everybody’s marketplace.

Then the quick round. ETH: “Programmable money.” Bitcoin: “Money that no one owns, but everybody can use.” The blockchain: “A source of truth.” And his message for builders: “Just keep building. If you know what you’re doing, keep learning and building and shipping.”
