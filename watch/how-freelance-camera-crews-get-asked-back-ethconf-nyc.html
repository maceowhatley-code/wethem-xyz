ETHConf: how freelance camera crews get asked back.

New York, 8 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

To quote this piece: “ETHConf: how freelance camera crews get asked back,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-06, https://wethem.xyz/watch/how-freelance-camera-crews-get-asked-back-ethconf-nyc. Each transcript line links to its second.

At ETHConf in Manhattan, June 2026, Maceo meets three freelancers filming a CEO around New York with a Leica SL3S on SmallRig rails and a V-mount battery (“It’s my caboose”). Asked how a small media team breaks into client work, Jacob gives the credit to Sire, who works at a camera store: “the client actually came into the store, and I just started like chatting him up,” and the conversation became a relationship, “now my fourth or fifth job with them.” What gets you asked back, Jacob says, is quality work and then something more: “being the type of person that someone wants to ask you to come back for,” someone “documenting with integrity” whom the client does not have to think about all day. He went full-time freelance two years ago, photo first, video since, and describes the difference (“Photo, you can be nimble and quick. Video, you try to be nimble and quick”) and how the edit shapes the shoot. They give their names at the end: Jacob Feingold, Shawn P. Bruce, spelled out, and Sire Roman, “Project Roman.”
