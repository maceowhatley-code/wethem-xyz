A family meal and a vibe check at the venue.

Sharon, Annabolo & Humpty, Denver, 4 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

To quote this piece: Sharon, Annabolo & Humpty, “A family meal and a vibe check at the venue,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/east-denver-vibe-check-eth-denver-2026-family-meal-denver. Each transcript line links to its second.

Three and a half minutes behind the scenes at ETH Denver 2026, filmed on the move at what the crew call a family meal. “Family meal. You guys call this family meal”, the man with the mic says, works out where he sits in the household, “We’re like the Jackson 3 now”, and then sets the segment: “ETH Denver vibe check. The vibes are amazing.”

The roll call is the first minute. “so who we here with today?” gets three answers, “Sharon”, then “Anabolo, drinking coffee”, then Humpty, whose introduction is a refusal to introduce him: “That’s it, just Humpty. He like Cher”, and “If you know Humpty, you know.” A shout goes out to Real Motion and “shout out to on-chain creators”.

The question underneath the segment is whether the week was any good. “A lot of people been talking a lot of smack”, the host says, and asks “what’s really the vibes here at ETH Denver?” His own answer starts with Sharon. “Sharon’s the vibes. You were the vibes”, he says, she was there at the start of the day and the end of it, she contributed to the rally several times, “she has dripped out on like nouns gear”, and “If you are in this space, everybody knows Sharon.”

Sharon’s own verdict is the shortest thing on the tape and the least sentimental. “No, it was a little shorter. I wanted more before I knew it was over”, she says of this year, and when he asks whether she wanted more, “Always. I love the art. I love that Nouns popped up and did your project.”

The last stretch is a case for the room itself. “this venue is, is something else”, he says, and does not pretend it is polished: “I’m saying it’s very grassroots. I feel like a lot of crypto people would never come to something like this.” He gives the origin as a decision three people made, that when he, David and Rob came together “we wanted to create a space for creatives to have a good time, get to know people, get to have a vibe and not feel like pressure”, instead of the usual round of what do you do and who are you. What that looks like on the night is people painting, “We’re writing on walls”, dabs going round, and a tray of food nobody can name until David does: “Yeah, it’s a Polish dumpling. We got Polish dumplings.” Then a group shot, and a line about how it looks from behind the camera.
