ETHConf: an Ethereum builder on his plan to run for office.

New York, 11 min · filmed June 2026

To quote this piece: “ETHConf: an Ethereum builder on his plan to run for office,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-06, https://wethem.xyz/watch/an-ethereum-builder-wants-to-run-for-office-ethconf-nyc. Each transcript line links to its second.

At ETHConf in Manhattan, June 2026, an Ethereum builder of several years tells Maceo he came “to feel out the ecosystem and see what folks are building.” Asked what a degen is, he says “Degen is like somebody who’s a gambler,” but “a lot of degen is not gambling.” On the community: “a lot of us are nomads, right? And our home base is, well, not so much for me anymore, but our home base is an event like this.” Then the turn: “I’ve really been looking at trying to, uh, run for local government,” pointing to “a record number of folks retiring,” among them “more than 50 Congress members who are retiring this year, and that is really unprecedented,” and a chance for something new: “as soon as there’s a viable third party, I think people might start rallying around it.” His message for the ecosystem: “I have not HODL’d, and I wish I had,” and plainly, “don’t blindly follow any kind of advice.” On what outsiders get wrong: “It’s not just finance bros and it’s not just tech bros. It is quite a diverse ecosystem.”
