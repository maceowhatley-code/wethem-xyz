Fatou, in line for Kai Cenat’s Streamer University auditions in New York, has more than 300 videos of herself on her phone. She has always wanted to be a content creator and was always afraid of being seen, so standing in the Streamer University line counts as the first public step, and she says it counts whether she gets in or not. Before her, June Anthony, who makes music and was homeless a year ago, and Noelle, who found building a platform in Nigeria competitive and started vlogging in New York to let her personality out.

Filmed in June 2026. The transcript below is from the original audio.
