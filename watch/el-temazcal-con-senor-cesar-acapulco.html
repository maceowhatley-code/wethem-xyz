El Temascal con Señor César.

Reported by Maritza, Acapulco, Mexico, 5 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

To quote this piece: “El Temascal con Señor César,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/el-temazcal-con-senor-cesar-acapulco. Each transcript line links to its second.

A temazcal practitioner in Acapulco explains how he came to the tradition through his family’s illness, why he kept going when they did not, and what is lost when knowledge lives only in the mouths of the people who hold it. In Spanish.

The temazcal is a Mesoamerican sweat lodge, and Señor César opens with the proverb the practice travels with: “Para todo mal, mezcal,” they say. He revises it. “Yo digo que para todo mal, un buen temazcal. Y para todo bien, mejor.”

He came to it through his family’s health. His wife started and did not continue; he did. “Mi familia no me siguió, pero yo sigo en la lucha.” What it gave him was the capacity to carry them: “Eso me ha fortalecido, porque ahora ellos siguen enfermos y yo los apoyo.” He puts it plainly, on being the one who cannot fall over: “No me puedo derrumbar, tengo que ser fuerte.”

The part that turns it from a profile into something urgent is what he says about the knowledge itself. “Este conocimiento es inmenso y no lo encuentras en un libro. Son episodios de la vida.”

Which is why he agreed to be filmed. He draws the line himself from a camera back to the codices: writing things down is what his ancestors did, and this is the version available now. “La difusión de usar la tecnología ahora para fines educativos.” What worries him is the gap in between. “Casi todo es de boca en boca, experiencia de boca en boca.”

Filmed by Maritza in Acapulco, Mexico. The transcript below is his own words in Spanish rather than a translation.
