Six months after quitting the nine to five.

Tomás, Online, 3 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

To quote this piece: Tomás, “Six months after quitting the nine to five,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-06, https://wethem.xyz/watch/leaving-a-nine-to-five-to-build-in-blockchain. Each transcript line links to its second.

What does it take to leave a 9-to-5 and build in blockchain full time? Tomas, 27, from Argentina, says: “Six months ago, I quit my nine to five job and I went all in building in blockchain.” He is building a startup called Aqua Zero with 1inch and Uniswap as partners.

The pitch: “Liquidity pools today are so broken. So we are making them more efficient.” Assets go into a shared pool and “you can provide liquidity to multiple pools in multiple chains simultaneously.” When a trade comes in, “the swap executes, and then the liquidity goes back to the shared liquidity pool, ready for the next swap in any pool, in any chain.”

On Ethereum: “I am really, really, really bullish on Ethereum,” partly because “Ethereum is playing like the long-term game” with brokers and stablecoins. Three minutes, filmed on the road.
