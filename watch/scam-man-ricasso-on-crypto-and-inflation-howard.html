Scam, man: Ricasso on crypto and inflation.

Washington, DC, 13 min, filmed 25 September 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

To quote this piece: “Scam, man: Ricasso on crypto and inflation,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-09-25, https://wethem.xyz/watch/scam-man-ricasso-on-crypto-and-inflation-howard. Each transcript line links to its second.

On Day 1 of the Black Blockchain Summit 2026, Maceo sits down at Howard University with Eric Holder, known as Ricasso, a painter and muralist in DC. Ricasso talks first about the mural scene: opportunities “range and vary, just depending on how much you applying, how much you really doing. Who you know.” Asked where a newcomer should go to understand the city, he says: “Y’all stay uptown.” He means the Northwest side: “That’s where they doing all the gentrification and all that, like the transplant. Y’all stay up there.” Maceo shares his own path into crypto journalism and NFTs, then asks what Ricasso thinks of Bitcoin. His answer comes fast: “Scam, man.” He says he has never researched it and does not plan to until he has time. Maceo walks him through inflation and the dollar’s reserve position. Ricasso keeps pushing back: dollars and Bitcoin, he argues, are “the same devil, just a different format,” a new owner selling the same power. The conversation closes on Maceo’s own question, “Y’all getting down with the crypto?”, and Ricasso’s answer to the crew’s closing question: “Hey look, somebody gotta be them.”
