Frank on thirty years at Purple Haze and the art of glass.

To quote this piece: Frank, “Frank on thirty years at Purple Haze and the art of glass,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-03-27, https://wethem.xyz/watch/thirty-years-of-a-local-head-shop-and-why-community. Each transcript line links to its second.

Why will the man who runs vendor night at a Denver head shop not work with big cannabis? Frank works at Purple Haze, which has been open thirty years, and Final Friday is when local vendors set up on the last Friday of the month to hang out and show what they make.

He got the job through glass: “My love of glass and knowing glass artists got me the opportunity to start working here.” On why he keeps saying community: “Working here for so long, I know everybody in the hood. It’s sick to connect with people just over a vape or papers or a bong, and building that relationship just off of glass or weed.”

The event started with his vintage clothing: “This started because I wanted to sell my stuff, and I just wanted a space that I could do it, and it turned into this.” He blows glass himself, four or five years in, and says you get back what you put in. Denver, 2026.
