A sociology student on loneliness, capitalism, and what worries her most.

Edward, Reported by Maceo, Denver, 10 min · filmed 26 March 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Answers What is the most important global issue today? and If you could ask people around the world one question, what would you ask them?, see how other cities answered

What is the most pressing issue in the world? Edward, a sociology student in Denver, gives three. The first is education inequality: “Education is like the pipeline for social mobility, especially in our country. But there’s these power structures in place that make it impossible for many people to get access.” And: “My family’s military. That’s the only reason I get to go to school.”

The third is the globalisation of capitalism: “Capitalism, when it spread around the world, starts to incentivise people to make money instead of being kind of more human. Well, I don’t think humans are meant to be on this planet to compete with each other. We’re social beings to begin with.”

He points down the street: “I don’t think there should be a world where that gentleman doesn’t feel like he has the opportunity to go somewhere, or be able to find a place to sleep at night, or find comfort in community.”

What he does about it: he is president of the sociology club at the University of Colorado Denver, and got the school to fund about $2,500 of supplies for a local harm reduction organisation.
