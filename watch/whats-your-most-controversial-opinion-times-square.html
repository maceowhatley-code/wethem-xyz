What's your most controversial opinion? Times Square.

Reported by Maceo & Isaac · New York · 28 min

Twenty-eight minutes in Times Square asking one question, what’s your most controversial opinion, with Isaac on camera. The answers run from pineapple on pizza to Iran, and the piece keeps all of them.

The setup is the whole method in a sentence: “We show up, we shoot wherever we’re at, and today we’re shooting content for Deech and asking people what’s their hot take.” The early answers are the light ones. Summer should be five or six months long. Times Square is too crowded, “especially on the weekends.” Cereal first or milk first. A woman from Birmingham, Alabama, who wants the world to know her city is underrated: “Food scene, incredible.”

Then the takes with weight. A man who thinks the world will eventually turn on America: “We don’t want no more wars for Wall Street.” A Canadian who says he wants the annexation talk to happen, because “there’s more opportunity in the U.S. than there is Canada.” Trump from both directions across the afternoon, including a man who worked with refugees and immigrants and thinks the pipeline “Been Piled Up,” and a former ticket seller who remembers the man outside Trump Tower: “Every time I seen him, he gave me a hundred dollar bill.”

An educator and mother asks the crowd her own question, why people “put more time, energy, money, and love into animals than in human beings,” and gets an answer worth the walk: “we are challenging, we have an opinion, we talk back.”

There are takes here the site does not share and does not soften: on hookup culture, on who should drive, on gay rights, on Israel, and from a veteran of Iraq and Afghanistan on bombing Iran. They are on the tape because they were said in the square, which is the point of the question. A stretch in Spanish and a statement in Korean are transcribed as spoken.

Filmed by Maceo with Isaac in New York, June 2026, for the DEGEN and POV Market collaboration named in the video’s own description.
