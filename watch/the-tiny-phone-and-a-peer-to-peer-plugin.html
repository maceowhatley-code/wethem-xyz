John and an OG Pizza DAO member, shot on an iPad from the floor.

Reported by Maceo, Philadelphia, 5 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Why would anyone buy the smallest Android phone on the market? A Pizza DAO member says it is because it is bad at the internet: “It doesn’t work for scrolling content. So I can’t doom scroll on Reddit. I can’t browse crypto Twitter.” He adds: “It makes a phone less addicting and less interesting.”

He still gets sent links, and Signal has a limit: “You can only link five devices. If I have more than five devices, I literally can’t link all my devices through Signal even if I wanted to.” So he wrote a browser plugin that syncs text between his devices peer to peer: “It never stores your data, it just helps devices make connections to each other.”

His line for the interviewer: “This will make you realise how much screen space you’re wasting.” Filmed in Philadelphia at a Pizza Day party.
