Live Since 14, Music Is All He's Done

Ryan W. Miller, Reported by Maceo, Ellsworth, ME, 14 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

To quote this piece: Ryan W. Miller, “Live Since 14, Music Is All He's Done,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/live-since-14-music-is-all-hes-done-ellsworth. Each transcript line links to its second.

Ryan W. Miller, singer of OPEN X, on a life given only to music, right after the band’s set at ZAOstock in Ellsworth.

He tells Maceo he has played live since he was 14, after watching his father pull a guitar out of the basement. He talks about New York, the tours he says he played with Seether and Evanescence, and his advice to young musicians: “Be yourself.” He met Zaal through Facebook Messenger, and Zaal came to one of his solo shows. Then Maceo asks about crypto, and Ryan talks about XRP, patience, and meme coins: “It basically is the casino.” Later: why so many OPEN X songs deal with addiction among friends and family, a plan to tour hard for three years, and fans in Spain, New Zealand and Brazil.

For the One Question, he would ask people where they are from and why they like music. Then his own answer on what he has done with his life: “Music. That’s all I’ve done in my life is music.”
