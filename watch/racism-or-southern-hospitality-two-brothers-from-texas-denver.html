Racism or Southern hospitality? Two brothers from Texas.

Reported by Maceo, Denver, 11 min, filmed 21 March 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Answers What is the most important global issue today? and What is Ethereum? and If you could ask people around the world one question, what would you ask them?, see how other cities answered

Denver, March 2026, in front of a graffiti wall, two brothers from Texas, engineers, one of whom lives here now. The question of the week is the biggest global issue. The older brother: “people just got to let people live. And, like, right now there’s a lot of people encroaching on other people’s shit.” The younger: “We’re so focused on what divides us That it causes so much conflict in the world,” and if people focused on what they have in common, “A lot of those issues would resolve themselves Maybe that’s an idealistic approach.”

Then Texas, and the piece finds its subject. The younger brother: “a lot of people will confuse Southern, like, hospitality with, like, everyone’s just, like, nice towards each other, but I don’t think that necessarily means that you’re fully accepted. in every single community in the” The older: “Southern hospitality sometimes is just like a means of like masking other issues” and “you grow up not white in the South,” and only later “you’re like, damn, that person was actually kind of racist.” The memory, from COVID: wearing a mask at a YMCA, harassed by a man who said masks spread the virus, clapping back, and being the one thrown out, “just for like standing on business.” Compared with the Northeast, where people are “randos on the street” but will not come at you like that. Unprompted, they place the format: “you remind me of Channel 5 News.”

The last question is what is Ethereum, and neither knows. “Yo, I don’t even know what that word means” So they reason from the word: “I’m assuming it corresponds to like ethereal,” something spiritual that binds people together, and then, “Ethereum is your experience, your human experience in the universe. It’s so uniquely you.” Maceo gives them the real answer at the end, and one of them admits he looked it up mid-sentence. Names were not given. Language is unfiltered.
