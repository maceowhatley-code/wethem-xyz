Prison is a young man’s game.

Reported by Maceo, Denver, 15 min, filmed 26 March 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Fifteen minutes on a street with a man who has been to prison three times, and who opens by doing the arithmetic himself. “About being in prison, three different times in three different states, for 18 months apiece.”

What makes it unusual is that he is not telling it as a warning or a boast. He is assessing it, out loud, as a period of his life with a shape. “I didn’t go to prison until I was 30, I was a late bloomer, you know? But every 10 years, I go back for a year and a half.”

The line the piece is named for is his own summary of why that stopped: “besides prison is a young man’s game.”

The recording is also an honest document of what filming on a street is actually like. Halfway through a sentence about a prison detail in Osage County, someone he knows walks up and asks for a ride, and the interview simply waits, “Hey, what’s going on? How are we doing?”, before picking up where it left off. That is left in.

He mentions getting his GED inside, and the conversation moves through what the years since have been for.

The language is his own throughout and is published as spoken.
