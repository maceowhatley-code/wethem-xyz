There's no Black genre: Donna Maywar at the Trenton Book Fair.

Trenton, 10 min, filmed 10 October 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

To quote this piece: “There's no Black genre: Donna Maywar at the Trenton Book Fair,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-10-10, https://wethem.xyz/watch/there-is-no-black-genre-trenton-book-fair. Each transcript line links to its second.

Why dedicate a book fair to the Black writer? Donna Maywar, who owns Classics Used and Rare Books on West Lafayette Street in downtown Trenton with her husband, says: “Black people write every genre. There’s no Black genre. There’s science fiction, there’s comics, there’s mystery. We write every genre and we read every genre.” And even though it is the Year of the Black Writer, “everyone is invited.”

The bookshop, in business 25 years and downtown for 21, is “a community hub”: “We’ve had weddings in the bookshop. We host game nights. We play Scrabble. We play mahjong. We host local authors,” and “local kids don’t pay for books, as many as they can carry.”

Born and raised in Trenton, she remembers when “we had Black-owned businesses in my neighborhood”: a fish seller on Calhoun Street, corner stores, Black teachers, “examples of ourselves.” Today she points to Skil-Lit Cafe and the Sunrise Luncheonette on Warren Street, “still making breakfast and lunch and serving it with a smile.”

Asked what she would ask people around the world: “what is your dream and what are you doing?” Hers is her family, the bookshop and this fair: “I’m living my dream right now.” The video ends with a bonus lesson on crypto: what Bitcoin is, how inflation works, and what happens if people around the world stop holding dollars.

Filmed 10 October 2026 at the Trenton Book Fair in Trenton, New Jersey.
