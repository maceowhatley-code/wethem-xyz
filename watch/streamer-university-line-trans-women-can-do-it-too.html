Streamer University line: trans women can do it too.

New York, 8 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Three creators hand the microphone to each other outside Kai Cenat’s Streamer University auditions in New York. The first came because her chat made her and is closing in on a 54-week streak. The second, from the Bronx, nearly stayed home because she felt too old and let people talk her out of it. She came anyway, to show that trans women can do what anybody else does, and she has decided the view count will not be what settles it. The videos go in the archive for whoever finds them later. The last, from Washington Heights, makes music and came for a chance.
