Andrew Callaghan’s Carnival Tour hits Denver: fans on journalism.

Reported by Maceo · Denver · 14 min · filmed 4 April 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Answers If you could ask people around the world one question, what would you ask them? · see how other cities answered →

What does Andrew Callaghan do that other journalists do not? Fans in the line outside his Channel 5 show in Denver answer. “I like that he shows up where people are at and he talks to them,” one says. “He interviews like homeless people and just like regular people. You don’t see that a lot.”

David drove thirteen hours from Fargo for his birthday, and his question for the world: “Are you living a life for yourself or are you living it for other people?”

A young woman on grief wishes America had something like Dia de los Muertos, a day and a place to honour the dead. On losing her grandfather: “It was just like it was a beautiful sunny day and the world just kept moving even though my world was ending.” A man in a wheelchair calls the gunshot that put him there a head start. At the end the filmmaker is on stage with the host.
