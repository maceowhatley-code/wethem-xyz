Baby steps into crypto, with young mentors.

Washington, DC, 23 min, filmed 21 September 2024

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Executive producer: C.Y. Lee

On Day 2 of the Black Blockchain Summit 2024 at Howard University in Washington, DC, Maceo sits down at an outdoor table with Wanda, who taught herself crypto, for a conversation he says is “not even a formal interview.” She first heard of Bitcoin on a community radio show, where two brothers said “we needed to get in it early, right now,” and she wanted to be ahead of it: “I don’t want to get caught behind it. I want to get caught in front of it.” Caring for her parents came first. Around 2020 she started studying on her own, “it wasn’t so much about the money, it was more about the technology for me,” and the hard part was the first step: “Where do I start?” She trades a little, in “incy-wincy baby steps, because I don’t want to lose money,” and her teachers are young: “I follow young people now. I really do.” Her rule is patience, research and a mentor: “You really need mentorship.” Then her own story: a salesperson at her grandmother’s stand in a DC market at four, her own vending business, theater, cosmetology, real estate, and the years she gave her parents, which she “would never change it for the world.” Children, grandchildren, parents, “took care of them all. Now it’s all about me.” Maceo says what he is after, “helping Black people become first adopters of this technology,” and describes the We Them Media Discord as a place where “we are holding space for one another.” A note on the facts: the first Black Blockchain Summit was in September 2018 and 2024’s was the 7th, at Howard University (the summit’s own events page); US coins and currency are legal tender (31 U.S.C. 5103) and the IRS treats bitcoin as property (Notice 2014-21); on 15 August 1971 foreign governments could no longer exchange dollars for gold (Federal Reserve History); the SEC says day traders typically suffer severe losses in their first months; and since this was filmed, Executive Order 14178 (2025) bars US agencies from issuing a central bank digital currency.
