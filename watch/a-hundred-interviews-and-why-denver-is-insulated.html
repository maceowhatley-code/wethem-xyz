To quote this piece: “100 interviews before leaving Denver, and building a global network,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-03-12, https://wethem.xyz/watch/a-hundred-interviews-and-why-denver-is-insulated. Each transcript line links to its second.

How many interviews before leaving Denver? Maceo says a hundred, twenty-nine posted so far, to a couple on East Colfax who have never been interviewed. “I’m trying to get a friend in Ghana to do it. We have a guy in Nigeria.”

Shannon is from the Bay Area and says Denver is not so different, “since everyone from California moved here.” Jake grew up in Littleton and describes the state’s divide: “Denver is really insulated. The people who live in Denver just don’t really consider the rest of the state in their politics. The rest of the state is kind of rural, aside from the ski towns.”

The week’s question was whether the internet is good for us. Jake: “You used to have to go to the library to learn anything. You can look up on YouTube how to fix your car. We’re at a point where you could literally learn to do anything.” The couple end on the crypto question nobody in the industry answers: why? East Colfax, Denver.
