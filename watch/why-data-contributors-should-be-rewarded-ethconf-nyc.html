ETHConf: why data contributors should be rewarded.

New York · 7 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week →

At ETHConf in Manhattan, June 2026, a core contributor at OpenLedger explains what the company does: “OpenLedger’s biggest focus is to enable people to build custom AI models and agents,” built on the idea that “people who own data and can contribute data to build any kind of intelligence have to be rewarded.” Ram, who grew up between Singapore and India, describes why he likes blockchain: “Anyone could hold a wallet.” “It could be someone who’s in Nigeria who’s like trying to survive, trying to grow from there,” he says, or “it could be a billionaire sitting in New York who wants to make sure that his assets are protected.” “Everyone is pretty much using the same blockchain,” he adds. Asked what outsiders get wrong, he says “crypto is more than that,” and names his worry: “if there is anything which happens wrong in the industry, if there’s any manipulation at play or something like that, it gives a very bad narrative to the industry.” His message for builders in a bear market: “Keep building.” “One single person can build a great company out there,” he says. “I think it’s the best time ever.” A note on the facts: the record behind it includes a March 2025 order setting up a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve (White House) and the GENIUS Act of July 2025, the first federal rules for payment stablecoins.

Mentioned in this video: OpenLedger.
