Three friends on Denver, and what made them smile.

Derek, Andrew & Luis, Reported by Maceo, Denver, 5 min, filmed 3 April 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Answers What made you smile today?, see how other cities answered

Five and a half minutes at First Friday in Denver with a group of friends on the sidewalk, three of whom take the mic. Maceo introduces himself, says he is from Philly, and asks what they like about it here.

Derek Hand answers small and local: “I like just like the art that they have here”, the people, “the Lime scooters for sure”, and the cars. Andrew widens it to the map and makes the case Denver people make: “You got the mountains 30 minutes away, you got the plains 30 minutes away, you got everything you need right here.” What stands out is the range: “this really dope art district” one way and “diehard fans for all the sports teams” the other. “we ride for our sports teams. I don’t think no other city does that”, he says, and lands on there being “something for everybody”.

Derek’s second answer is safety, and he is sheepish about it. “I honestly think the safety as well”, he says, meaning “being able to just like ride scooters or like just go around Denver having fun”. The people, he reckons, are “kind of all in the same, like, mindset”. He is a snowboard instructor, and his version of the mountains is about the option: “you could go up to where you want to be alone”, so “you could be anywhere for any mindset, any personality”.

Then the weekly question. The project gets explained on tape, “this project called Picture It Didn’t Happen”, a crypto bounty board that puts up a question a week and pays a hundred dollars for the favourite submission, with entries in from India, Nigeria, Uganda and Kenya. This week: what made you smile.

Andrew goes home for it. He is at school in Nebraska, and what made him smile was “coming back home and being able to be with my guys”, the ones he has known “since middle school, high school, kindergarten”. “The happiest that I am is, is at home with my family.”

Derek’s answer is smaller and more honest: “getting out of my comfort zone about coming out with the, like, my friends again”, because he is “usually homebody a little bit”. What he takes from the night is “making sure that every moment’s special to you”.

Luis, pulled in from the edge of the group, has the same answer plus a visitor: his friends are back in town and “My grandpa’s visiting in town from Mexico”, from Chihuahua. He puts the rest of it plainly: “Again, to just be around my family. I’m healthy.”

Asked what they would ask the world, Derek wants to know whether people have snowboarded, “That’s my main passion.” Andrew wants the reason people get out of bed, “what’s their why?”, because the answers differ enough to be worth hearing. Luis asks the one that stays with you, “where do you feel the safest”, and what “brings joy and passion to your life”. Then they go back to their night.
