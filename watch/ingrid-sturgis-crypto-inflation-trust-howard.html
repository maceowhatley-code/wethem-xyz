A journalism professor on crypto and inflation.

Washington, DC, 16 min, filmed 25 September 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

On Day 1 of the Black Blockchain Summit 2026, Maceo Whatley talks with Ingrid Sturgis, who teaches journalism at Howard University’s School of Communications and still works as a journalist herself. She describes crypto as a currency owned by no single entity, one she says can be exploited: “It is a casino,” and some of the people trading in it are, in her word, “unscrupulous.” Maceo walks her through Bitcoin as a hedge against inflation and the idea of de-dollarization; she is candid that she is new to some of it, but firm that current tariffs and trade policy are already weakening the US dollar’s position. The conversation turns personal when Maceo asks whether she uses the word in his old podcast’s name. Her answer is “No,” a stance she says she settled on in college. She closes with advice: for anyone navigating poverty, think about becoming an entrepreneur; for Black students who want to be journalists, learn the craft and stay honest. Filmed at Howard University in Washington, DC, on 25 September 2026.
