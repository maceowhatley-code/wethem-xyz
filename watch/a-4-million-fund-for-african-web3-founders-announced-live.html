A $4 million fund for African Web3 founders, announced live.

Online, 13 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Executive producer: C.Y. Lee

To quote this piece: “A $4 million fund for African Web3 founders, announced live,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/a-4-million-fund-for-african-web3-founders-announced-live. Each transcript line links to its second.

Why back African Web3 founders with grants and equity instead of tying them to one chain? In this live stream session, the first speaker announces Africa Ventures, “a $4 million fund exclusively focusing on African Web3 founders.” Depending on the stage of the business, it goes from grants up to $150,000 to equity financing up to half a million dollars, with engineers inside the fund for hands-on support. The first speaker stresses: “It is not linked to StarkNet only,” so a founder building on Base, Lisk, Scroll or anything else counts.

The first speaker’s reasoning is what funders told the fund: it is hard to see how money put into Africa turns into value, and investors end up backing people rather than the business, so founders who do not speak the language VCs are used to often go unbacked.

A second speaker adds that founders have to “meet people halfway” by adapting to how funders think, and asks protocols to stop telling builders “please come and build on my chain.” The line: “We have to empower our entrepreneurs from Africa to learn so many skills that they become bankable.” That speaker points to local groups, the Web3 clubs of Nairobi and Web3 Bridge in Nigeria, as the ones on the ground who can carry that support.

Streamed live in September 2025.
