ETHConf: a software engineer looks under DeFi's yield.

New York, 4 min · filmed June 2026

To quote this piece: “ETHConf: a software engineer looks under DeFi's yield,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-06, https://wethem.xyz/watch/a-software-engineer-looks-under-defis-yield-ethconf-nyc. Each transcript line links to its second.

At the first ETHConf in Manhattan, June 2026, a software engineer at a traditional finance information company comes to look, not to pitch. Why she came: “I’m personally pretty interested in blockchain and all the cryptocurrency-related topics. So I just want to see what people are working on right now, possibly look for some future opportunities.” What impressed her: “I think the most impressive thing is how like DeFi is working now.” What she does not yet understand: “I just see like, okay, I can like put the money there and get this yield, but I don’t know what’s exactly going on.” She is a software engineer already, in a different field: “this is a totally different type of engineering.” Where she stands with it: “I do work in finance, but like it’s more on the engineering part. I don’t exactly know like how to generate value with this. I think it’s just like, I see this as a shortage that I need to learn more about right now before I can really make something impressive.” She has an idea of her own, games on blockchain, which she calls too niche on its own: “I have some ideas about like applications on blockchain, but it’s mainly just like, you know, games.” Maceo’s answer points at composability: “the goal is for like blockchains have to be composable, right? So if your game can leverage DeFi products in a meaningful way that actually create value and create a sustained economy.”
