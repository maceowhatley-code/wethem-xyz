What made you smile today? That is the week’s question, asked on the corner of Santa Fe on a First Friday in Denver, in the middle of a freestyle cipher.

One rapper, early in a verse: “If you look at my life, it might be tragic, but I had fun every single day.” Maceo takes the mic and delivers the bounty announcement as a verse: anyone around the globe can claim it, go outside, ask some questions, do it on video.

Asked off the mic, a rapper answers in the next freestyle: “Make art with my friends.” A visitor named Sean, who has never been to Denver before, gives the simplest answer: “Denver made me smile.”

Nineteen minutes. The last verse is violent in its content, and the language is unfiltered; the transcript is bleeped where the captions were.
