The biggest question of 2026.

Amaziah Williams, Washington, DC, 15 min, filmed 25 September 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

On Day 1 of the Black Blockchain Summit 2026, Maceo sits down on the Howard University campus in Washington, DC, with Amaziah Williams, a broadcast journalism major with a philosophy minor, born and raised in DC. Amaziah traces his path to journalism back to his great-grandmother, who graduated from Howard in 1943, and to sports broadcasting he picked up in high school. He says he does not know much about crypto beyond friends who trade Bitcoin, and worries about scams. Maceo walks him through inflation: the dollar’s value falling as more of it is printed, and why he calls that the biggest tax on working people. Amaziah pushes back with his own knowledge, pointing out that it is illegal for anyone but the Federal Reserve to print money, and Maceo agrees that counts as fraud. The two imagine what hyperinflation could look like for families already working two jobs. Amaziah closes with his own question for the world: what is the best way to handle inflation, which Maceo calls the biggest question of 2026. The conversation ends on the name behind Maceo’s brand, We Them Media, and the podcast name it came from, which Amaziah says he is fine with.
