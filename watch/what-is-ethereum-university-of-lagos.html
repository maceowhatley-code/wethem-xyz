What is Ethereum? UNILAG street interviews.

Bori, Reported by Kafani, Lagos, 10 min

To quote this piece: Bori, “What is Ethereum? UNILAG street interviews,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/what-is-ethereum-university-of-lagos. Each transcript line links to its second.

Kafani takes one question to the University of Lagos, finds that most students have never heard of Ethereum, and ends the video by setting up a stranger’s first wallet on camera and sending him five dollars.

The first student half-knows it. “Is it not, like, I don’t know, a cryptocurrency?” Then she tells a story that turns the piece serious. Years ago a friend told her mother to get involved, and she was the one who wrote the phrase down for her. They have lost it. What it was worth then and what it is worth now are not the same number. Kafani has to say the hard part out loud: you cannot recover a seed phrase once it is gone.

From there she teaches rather than tests. What the thing is for, what a wallet is, what it costs to start. “You can buy as low as one dollar worth.” She names the apps she would recommend and the ones she uses.

Two students have never heard the word at all. One has never heard of Web3 either, and offers Forex as the nearest thing she knows. A third, Bori, gets as far as “Is a money? Bitcoin money.”

The last one is the reason to watch it to the end. He has never heard of Ethereum, has no wallet, and cheerfully agrees he is “literally not a web three person”. So Kafani onboards him live: downloads the app, creates the account, and stops to deliver the only real warning in the video. Never share your seed phrase with anybody, ever. He picks a username, gets his first wallet, and she sends him five dollars in ETH.

She closes by reading out the textbook definition, having spent nine minutes demonstrating that a definition was never the useful part.
