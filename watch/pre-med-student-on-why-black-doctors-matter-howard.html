A pre-med student on why Black doctors matter.

Washington, DC, 8 min, filmed 25 September 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

To quote this piece: “A pre-med student on why Black doctors matter,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-09-25, https://wethem.xyz/watch/pre-med-student-on-why-black-doctors-matter-howard. Each transcript line links to its second.

At Howard University in Washington, DC, on Day 1 of the Black Blockchain Summit 2026, Maceo sits down with Rosh, a Howard second-year majoring in biology on the pre-med track. Rosh, who is from Chicago, explains why he wants to become a Black surgeon: Black doctors make up only 4 percent of the field, he says, and he wants to be part of changing that. He has never had a Black doctor himself, only a Black dentist. He is weighing Howard’s own medical school against the University of Chicago and Meharry, with a preference for an HBCU. Rosh introduces three friends: Parker; Jelani, a health science major; and a finance major. Each describes what little or how much they know about crypto, from dividend stocks to the basics of the blockchain. Maceo, who says he has covered crypto for five years, walks the group through inflation, the printing of money, and de-dollarization, the idea that other countries are losing confidence in the US dollar. One friend calls the possible fallout catastrophic. The video closes on the group’s own question for the world: Did you get 1% better than you was yesterday?
