A freshman learns why the dollar is losing value.

Washington, DC, 8 min, filmed 25 September 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Carmelo, a freshman at Howard University in Washington, DC, studying philosophy, talks with Maceo of We Them Media on Day 1 of the Black Blockchain Summit 2026, held on Howard’s campus on 25 September 2026. He explains that he chose philosophy over psychology because he prefers logic and reasoning to the emotional side of things. Maceo, who studied moral psychology before spending years studying cryptocurrency, walks him through inflation: what it is, why governments print money, and why the US dollar has served as the world’s reserve currency. Carmelo restates the mechanism correctly, that printing money steadily decreases the dollar’s value, and says the effect on people already struggling would look like living in the slums. He also talks about Black Americans losing their history and authenticity to entertainment and celebrity culture, and says he wants to be someone who makes change. The two close with the crew’s own closing question, which Carmelo answers yes to. He tells Maceo he probably will not attend next year’s summit, though he leaves the door open.
