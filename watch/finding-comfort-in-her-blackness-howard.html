Comfortable in her Blackness: a senior's story.

Washington, DC, 9 min, filmed 25 September 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

To quote this piece: “Comfortable in her Blackness: a senior's story,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-09-25, https://wethem.xyz/watch/finding-comfort-in-her-blackness-howard. Each transcript line links to its second.

Molina Diaz is a senior at Howard University, studying philosophy and sociology, from Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Filmed on Day 1 of the Black Blockchain Summit 2026, Howard University, Washington, DC, 25 September 2026.

Molina grew up around mostly white people and says that changed at Howard, where being around people who look like her and think like her “made me comfortable in being who I am.” She says she is now “very comfortable just being in my Blackness.”

She says she does not know much about crypto beyond “an online type thing, like Bitcoin.” Maceo walks her through the blockchain, the gold standard and the dollar’s place as the world’s reserve currency, and what could happen if that changed. Molina turns the conversation back on him, asking “when are politicians and governments actually going to live up to their title or what they’re supposed to be doing?”

Her One Question: “What exactly are you doing in the world to make it a better place?” Her own answer is about the nonprofit work she has done, “bringing awareness to the refugee and humanitarian crisis” happening around the world. She closes with a message: “Keep doing you and keep being Black.”
