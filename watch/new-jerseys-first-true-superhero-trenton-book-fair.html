New Jersey's first true superhero: Eric Cooper at the Trenton Book Fair.

Eric Cooper, Trenton, 8 min, filmed 10 October 2026

To quote this piece: Eric Cooper, “New Jersey's first true superhero: Eric Cooper at the Trenton Book Fair,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-10-10, https://wethem.xyz/watch/new-jerseys-first-true-superhero-trenton-book-fair. Each transcript line links to its second.

Why write the nerdy sci-fi nobody expects from us? Eric Cooper, “a born and raised Trenton native in New Jersey,” “an author, writer, optician by trade and also chauffeur,” says: “Most people don’t think that we do the nerdy sci-fi stuff. We do.” His book is Knight Seeker, and he calls its hero “New Jersey’s first true superhero character”: the story starts in Trenton and branches to New York City, Philadelphia and Delaware.

He started writing “going through depression in my life,” and wrote the hero’s backstory from his own. Each book carries a test: the first is “about foundation,” the second “about your faith,” and the third is about morality.

On why: “always about sharing our stories, especially being Black.” “I’m sharing my depression. I’m sharing my triumphs. I’m sharing my issues.” Writing it “makes me feel whole again.”

He also runs Black Label Comic-Con, which “is about showing off other independent authors” and comic artists who “don’t work for the big companies.” The first show was in 2019, it ran in 2022 and 2023, and he is taking a break, maybe returning in 2027, “here in Trenton.”

Filmed 10 October 2026 at the Trenton Book Fair in Trenton, New Jersey.
