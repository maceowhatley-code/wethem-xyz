Why a first edition conference was built around products.

Mitong, Jos, 14 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Executive producer: C.Y. Lee

To quote this piece: Mitong, “Why a first edition conference was built around products,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/why-we-built-a-product-focused-conference-jos. Each transcript line links to its second.

Why a product focused conference in Nigeria? Mitong, the organizing lead of PRODFEST in Jos, Plateau State, explains in order why the first edition exists. It started with research in October: “what are the current existing three events that happen and how can we do something that is a bit different.”

His community had been about building since day one, so he widened the word: “content creators, marketers, product designers, they all they’re all builders and that is who we brought together.” And: “why not let’s do a product focused conference and that’s where the name Profest came from”, a mixture of products and festivaling.

Underneath it is an argument. He has heard the line that “Africans are not consumers of technology they are not creators”, that the continent is only there for airdrops and someone else’s funding. The event is his answer. Thirteen minutes, Jos.
