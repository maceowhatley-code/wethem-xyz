What made you smile today?

Reported by Kaspa · Campus · 9 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week →

Answers What made you smile today? · see how other cities answered →

One question, asked to a dozen students on a Nigerian campus: what actually made you smile today? The answers run from an exam that went well to simply being alive, and the person asking notices something about the question itself by the end.

The answers are short and specific. One says his mother. One says an exam, “because most of the questions is just what I read yesterday.” One will not narrow it down: “I’m alive, of course.” Another has had a flat day and says so, then finds something anyway.

The closing observation is the reason this piece is worth nine minutes. “Every single person that I asked what made you smile today has actually smiled first, before I showed that question. So if they are having a bad day, they think about what has actually made them smile. So this is a very thoughtful bounty.”

That is a working note about method, from a contributor discovering mid-shoot that the question does something to the person being asked before they answer it. It is the clearest description on the channel of why One Question is built the way it is.

Along the way he explains the programme to camera for anyone watching who might want to file their own: a weekly contest where you go out, interview people or take on a challenge, and get paid for what you bring back.
