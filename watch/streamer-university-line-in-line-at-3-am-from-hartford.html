Streamer University line: in line at 3 AM from Hartford.

New York, 4 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

They drove down from Hartford for Kai Cenat’s Streamer University auditions in New York and were on the sidewalk at three in the morning. At that hour the line only reached partway down the block. By the time the camera finds them it runs far past that. The man does most of the talking. He grew up going all in on whatever he and his cousin wanted, he says the woman they came with is the kind of person who rolls the car window down for someone asking for help, and to him Kai’s reach is every culture standing in the line around them.

Filmed in June 2026. The transcript below is from the original audio.
