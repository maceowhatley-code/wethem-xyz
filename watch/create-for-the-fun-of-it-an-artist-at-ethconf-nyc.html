ETHConf: create for the fun of it.

New York, 9 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

At the first ETHConf in Manhattan, June 2026, Shanelle Julia Rosita, a visual abstract artist from Brooklyn, sits down with Maceo beside her stickers and her art. She hosts workshops “for tech folks, people that are in high-stress environments to have a creative pause and to reflect and release and use art to transmute their feelings,” a practice that started at Miami Art Week (“Art Basel is its own thing. Miami Art Week is the big umbrella thing”). In a room full of things on autopilot, she says, “people are craving that like hands-on IRL experience.” Her own discipline is a daily drawing put out on Tezos, so she does not lose the craft of paper. Asked how New York keeps the Knicks in 5 mentality, her advice is “Just be New York. Just New Yorkers doing New York stuff. Nothing weird.” Asked what crypto is, she answers: “I’d say crypto is freedom,” an opportunity “to challenge and question the way things have been going.” Her message for the builders: “Don’t forget to create just for the fun of it.” A year from now, she hopes, Mexico, for the art scene. A note on the facts: Tezos is an open-source, proof-of-stake blockchain, launched in June 2018, that supports NFTs (Tezos documentation).
