Arikia Millikan, on licensing journalism peer to peer.

Reported by Maceo, New York, 15 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

CTRL+X is a blockchain-based peer-to-peer journalism platform built to address the collapse of the media industry’s business model. Founder Arikia Millikan explains how it enables direct reader-to-journalist micropayments, protecting creators’ work from AI scraping and from centralized intermediaries.

“Publications are going out of business, databases are being deleted if nobody wants to pay for maintenance anymore. We’re losing troves of human historical knowledge,” Millikan warns. The platform removes the publication as a central intermediary, so authors maintain their own archives and control whether AI systems can access and buy their work.

“AI companies have largely just scraped the open web and stolen millions of journalistic assets that do have a value,” she explains. CTRL+X uses blockchain rails for transparent micropayments, where creators are paid directly by readers accessing individual articles.

Millikan brought 20 years of journalism experience to building this, after first envisioning it a decade ago as a research project. “This infrastructure has really been missing from the whole process. It’s gotten to a point where everything’s ready, and from the journalistic side, there’s no denying the need for something like this to exist anymore.”
