Carol on deaf rights, and her family taking on Hollywood’s ADA exemption.

Carol, Reported by Maceo, Philadelphia, 14 min

To quote this piece: Carol, “Carol on deaf rights, and her family taking on Hollywood’s ADA exemption,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/the-seven-year-fight-to-caption-hollywood. Each transcript line links to its second.

How long did it take to make Hollywood caption its films? Carol says roughly seven years, on paper petitions, from the late 1980s. At the time, she says, the film industry was the third largest in the country, behind oil and automobiles, and it had arranged not to be covered. “You couldn’t go to a damn American movie.”

“No such thing as the internet. No such thing as social media. Hash what?” When theatres complied, they scheduled captioned showings “at maybe ten at night on a Tuesday, or eleven in the morning on a Thursday.” That was the second fight: “Once we started the fight, we didn’t accept anything less than normal.”

She brought together organisations that had never spoken to each other, hard of hearing and deaf, by refusing to let the difference be the subject. Then she talks about what she is working on now. Philadelphia.
