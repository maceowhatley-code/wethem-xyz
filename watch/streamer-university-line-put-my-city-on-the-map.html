Streamer University line: put my city on the map.

At Kai Cenat’s Streamer University auditions in New York, an Albany creator wants to put his city on the map, because nobody big comes from there yet. A streamer from Southside Jamaica, Queens has been at it since 2019 and admits the numbers are not looking good. A man with a gaming lifestyle brand plans to wing whatever the audition turns out to be, since he came for the connections. The last word goes to a Christian content creator in a Colombia jersey who rates his excitement a ten.
