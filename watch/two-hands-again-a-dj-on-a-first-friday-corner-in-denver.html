Two hands again: a DJ on a First Friday corner in Denver.

DJ Bkellz, Denver, 7 min · filmed 3 April 2026

What made you smile this week? DJ Bkellz, playing a set on a corner at First Friday in Denver, says his favorite quote is “the only day wasted is the day that you don’t laugh.” He posts something he is grateful for on Instagram every day, and this week it included the artist Heilig, who makes “all these cool cats.” He has started gardening again, peas and beans for now: “Everyone should grow food. It’s easy. All it takes is dirt and water.”

He broke his wrist snowboarding and DJed one-handed, and everybody loved it: “wait till I got 2 hands again. Here I am with 2 hands, baby.” Raised in Watertown, New York, he threw music festivals at Woodhenge, a sustainable community where they built “an off-grid solar-powered underground house” with a music venue off the back. Then COVID came: he sold everything, bought a motorhome, traveled the country and ended up in Denver.

Last week’s question was the most important issue in the world today. His answer is that musicians are underpaid. He says Ireland recently passed a universal income for musicians, and he calls for “a union and a collective for musicians”: if he sells 100 tickets at $20 each, “why do I only get $100 and the venue gets $10,000?” He also invites people to a show at the Black Box that Sunday where festival tickets are hidden as Easter eggs.
