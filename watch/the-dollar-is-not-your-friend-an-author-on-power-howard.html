The dollar is not your friend: an author on power.

Dennis Walker, Washington, DC, 19 min, filmed 26 September 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

On Day 2 of the Black Blockchain Summit 2026, Maceo talks with Dennis Walker, at the summit for a soft launch of his new book, The Birth of Power: A Forward Vision for the Next Great Migration. Its premise: “as African Americans, our quest for power has been misdirected.” He defines power as “the ability to influence change that is both impactful and long-lasting,” and traces the book to a conversation with former Ambassador Andrew Young, who told him, in Walker’s account, “African Americans need to understand money, what it is, and how it works.” Asked about crypto as a casino, he says “I’d say you’re right. 99.999% of it is,” and then: “I’m not talking about crypto, I’m talking about Bitcoin.” His case against the dollar is simple: “The dollar is not your friend because the dollar is losing value every day.” He pictures where Black Americans started after slavery as a 100-yard dash run in a ball and chain against a 50-yard head start. He answers a question Maceo carried from Times Square about formerly colonized countries, and describes the Black sovereign wealth fund his book proposes. A note on the facts: a January 2020 dollar bought about 77 cents’ worth in August 2026 (BLS); the Fed’s measure ran under its 2 percent goal in 96 of 108 months from 2012 to 2020 and at 3.7 percent in July 2026 (BEA); US inflation peaked at 9.1 percent over the year ended June 2022, not in 2020 or 2021 (BLS), and his 12 percent is his own estimate; the GENIUS Act lets stablecoin reserves be cash, deposits or Treasuries due within 93 days, not bonds in general; Nigeria’s inflation peaked at 34.8 percent in December 2024 (Central Bank of Nigeria).
