A first reaction video: a Hebrew prayer shot in the Israeli desert.

Oryahh, Reported by Maceo, Online, 6 min

To quote this piece: Oryahh, “A first reaction video: a Hebrew prayer shot in the Israeli desert,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/a-first-reaction-video-a-hebrew-prayer-shot-in-the-israeli-desert. Each transcript line links to its second.

Maceo’s first reaction video, and the song is entirely in Hebrew. Maceo reacts to a music video from Oryahh, whom he met earlier that year through a friend, and whom he saw working on music in the evenings in a studio in Jerusalem: “it was just like kind of surreal to me, you know, to go into this foreign environment and see this dude working.” This is Oryahh’s first music video.

Maceo does not speak Hebrew, so he reacts to what he can see and hear: the voice (“All I hear is his beautiful voice”), a fire lit in the woods after dark that “feels symbolic,” like “the beginning of something,” and the tefillin Oryahh puts on to pray. Maceo says he is impressed by how professional the video looks.

Maceo closes by talking about what he learned of Oryahh’s path back to faith, calls Oryahh versatile, with hip-hop as well as more prayer-adjacent music, and says he is excited to see what Oryahh does next.
