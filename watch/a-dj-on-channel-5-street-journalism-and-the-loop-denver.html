A DJ on Channel 5, street work and the loop.

Kevin, Denver, 7 min, filmed 3 April 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Answers What made you smile today? and If you could ask people around the world one question, what would you ask them?, see how other cities answered

Seven minutes with a Denver DJ, taped at his setup at a First Friday, and for most of it the questions run both ways.

It opens on a sound he had just used. “I only use it at the end of every set”, he says, and then explains the mechanics of it: “When you go all the way up the feedback goes over 100 and it’ll just keep looping for however long you want it to.” Asked whether that goes into a wedding set, he laughs it off. He works the weddings; the spiral is for other nights.

Then he turns interviewer. He hears the man with the mic is out doing man on the street work and asks whether he knows Andrew Callaghan, who, it turns out, is in town that week. “he’s my favorite”, he says. He came for the entertainment, “I started watching him when he was doing the street content”, when it was “just pure entertainment at that point”, and stayed for the turn: “when he actually took, you know, his following and did something like productive for the world with it”. So he started paying, five dollars a month, “Started paying him five bucks a month for the Patreon”. His favourite piece is the border episode, where the reporter “snuck into Mexico and then he tried to sneak back into America” and ended up arrested, and what that proved is short: “he’s really out here”. His summary of the job is a working man’s: “that’s what it takes, you know, feet on the ground”.

He will not make the show, because “I have to DJ a wedding tomorrow”, which is the moment he works out why Callaghan is in Denver at all.

The middle of the tape is the DJ interviewing We Them Media. The weekly bounty gets explained, “definitely inspired by Channel 5”, with “we’ve been putting up like $100 a week” and entries so far from India, Nigeria, Kenya and Uganda. He wants the mechanics: how the winner gets picked, how the street content connects to the money. The answers are honest about being unfinished, “kind of vibing and trying to figure out what fair looks like”, and the crypto backstory that follows is a college one, a roommate, airdrops, and “I never had $8,000 before”.

Then the questions come back around. This week’s is what made you smile, and his answer needs no thinking about: dogs, because “pretty much every time I see a dog I smile”. His own question for the world is four words, “What are we doing, man”, and then he gives his name. Kevin. AKA Carbon.
