Who is Maceo? Interviewed by Daniel.

Maceo · Reported by Daniel · Abuja · 12 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week →

Executive producer: C.Y. Lee

Abuja, March 2025, and for once the camera is on Maceo. Daniel is asking, with Maceo coaching him through it, and the first two minutes are false starts over the name. Maceo’s answer to the question of who he is: “I’m a rapper. I’d say first and foremost,” then, “I’m always a community builder, first and foremost. Like I wouldn’t say I’m the best founder.” His guiding light, in his words: “how do I do it in a way that makes sure I’m doing everybody Who I feel is a part of this journey with me How do I do them justice?”

The road so far: “with funding from CY, my patron I’ve been able to travel to more than 12 different blockchain conferences, events around the globe, eight different countries now,” from Asia to Africa, Ethiopia the month before, now Nigeria again. The test of all of it, “what I’m building is creating value for the people who are aligned with me who are trying to build platforms that actually serve their communities and not just you know extract value from them.”

Then the definitions this site is built on. “We Them Media is a cooperatively run media company.” The podcast and the company: the podcast was him documenting us, and “We Them Media is us bringing us to market in a meaningful way.” The goal: “to both redefine the future of the creator economy using decentralized technologies and also to make sure that African products, African builders are getting to market irrespective of what chain they’re building on.” Why a DAO: “the goal is to build an international organization that can outlast me.”

He is candid about output. “people see us as a fake media company because we don’t put out videos very often. Yeah. And I respect that take, you know, because I don’t edit videos.” What changed was finding people who care about post-production: Superbio and the team in Nigeria, Yofi in Ethiopia, Aziz in Nairobi. Why anyone should trust him: “I’m community-minded You know, I’m wondering how what I’m building can serve you,” and “I want We The Media to feel like a place that, you know, you can safely go and be part of the new internet.” The shout-outs name the network this archive stands on: Creative Alchemy, “really the core community of my community,” 40 Acres DAO, Friends with Benefits “for getting me this camera, for getting me this tripod,” CY, Donna and Aja. And where he wants to get to: “no longer being Maceo, the guy who does 25% of it by himself.” Language is unfiltered. The transcript below is from the original audio.
