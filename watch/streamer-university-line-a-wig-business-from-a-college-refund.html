A wig stylist from North Jersey left her ID at home on the morning of Kai Cenat’s Streamer University auditions in New York. The story she tells in line is how the business started. She had a full scholarship, dropped out, and put the refund into wigs. Last year she quit her job to do hair full time, and this week she is buying the PC to start streaming. Before her, a streamer explains why he wants to get big, which is so he can hand smaller creators the kind of opportunity he sees Kai handing out.
