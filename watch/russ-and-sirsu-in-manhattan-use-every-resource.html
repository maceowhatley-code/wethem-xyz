Russ and Sirsu in Manhattan: use every resource at your disposal.

New York, 24 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

To quote this piece: “Russ and Sirsu in Manhattan: use every resource at your disposal,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/russ-and-sirsu-in-manhattan-use-every-resource. Each transcript line links to its second.

A walk through Manhattan at night and a long table afterwards, with Maceo filming: Worldwide Russ and Sirsu interview each other about what New York taught them. Russ sets it up: “I want to talk with people who made it in New York”.

Sirsu’s first lesson is the one the piece turns on: “use every resource at your disposal.” A cousin’s rent-stabilised apartment in Harlem lasted him six years, and he knew “everybody in the 6-block radius from all the restaurants, all the bars, all the libraries, all the amazing spots.” His rule for the city: if you have integrity and “you just offer to listen, you offer time, you offer space”, then “You can pay that multiple times over and get that back, even exponential rates.”

Russ came for Fashion Week and stayed: “You never know who you talking to”, and “if you can make it here, you can make it anywhere.” What he wants now: “I really just like to chase curiosities, bro.” Sirsu agrees that “curiosity, like, and spontaneity are like cousins”, and warns about digital tools “that can keep us in a way like our own prisons if we don’t find ways to to touch grass”.

Then Russ’s question: building a community online against building it in person. Sirsu: “if I start with one, I end with the other.” His project Bite Party, “essentially like collective coordination game where there’s a digital apple”, is the online half; the point is what it becomes in a room. Russ thinks “IRL, I think, has quicker trust building”. Sirsu answers with the history of their class of 2019 to 2021: “don’t sell yourself short”, the projects dropped when the money came in, and “we would talk about extraction and then get extracted at the same time.”

It closes on “the friends you met along the way”, on travel (“Once those veils are removed, it’s like you can’t go back no more”), and on the Eagles and the Sixers. Filmed by Maceo in Manhattan, New York, 22 September 2026. Language is unfiltered. The transcript below marks Russ as A and Sirsu as B; the one line marked C, at 20:24, could be either of them.
