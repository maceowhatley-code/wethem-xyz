The hope is bigger than the fear: crypto on the Metro.

Spanish, Washington, DC, 15 min, filmed 25 September 2026

On a moving Metro train in Washington, DC, Trey talks about growing up Jamaican and being read as Puerto Rican in America instead. He says people who meet him assume he is Spanish and give him that label before he speaks. This is Day 1 of the Black Blockchain Summit 2026 (Howard University, Washington, DC, 25 September 2026), and the ride becomes a lesson in blockchain, inflation, and why the dollar can lose value. Trey says he only knew crypto could make money before this conversation. He turns the lesson into his own read on gambling, calling it an addiction like drugs or drinking, and says the pull of hope is more dangerous than the fear of losing. Before the ride ends, he asks himself the day’s closing question, whether his ancestors would be proud of him, and answers it himself: the answer is never yes, because they always want him to do better, so he keeps going.
