Awareness, action, and what is missing between builders.

PK Stevens, Jos, 9 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Executive producer: C.Y. Lee

Where is blockchain in Africa now? PK Stevens, head of operations and co-founder of IHQ, answers at ProdFest in Jos, Plateau State, straight off stage from a talk on blockchain and Africa’s digital future. “We’re unlocking innovation through technology, infrastructure, and community.”

Maceo quotes a line from the company’s CTO the day before: not build for Africa but build from Africa. PK puts a structure under it: “we’re still in kind of the awareness phase.” The shift he wants is “we’re coming out of this phase of crypto is blockchain to blockchain is a platform.” His method is a room: he ran what he says was the first pop-up city across Africa, in Ghana, and another this year in Kenya. “get people in a space for a while and then change how they think about blockchain technology.”

He opened his talk on belief: “we don’t talk enough about people that believe in Africa and then can use the technology.” Nine minutes.
