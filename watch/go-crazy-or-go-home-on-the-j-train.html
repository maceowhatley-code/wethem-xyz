On the train after the Knicks won the finals.

Reported by Maceo & Isaac, New York, 8 min · filmed June 2026

To quote this piece: “On the train after the Knicks won the finals,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-06, https://wethem.xyz/watch/go-crazy-or-go-home-on-the-j-train. Each transcript line links to its second.

What does “go crazy or go home” mean? On the J train in New York the night the Knicks won, a rapper who goes by QuaGoCrazy says: “I got a motivational quote for the kids, my boy right here, the grown folks. It’s for the world. Go crazy or go home.” He means effort: “What I mean by go crazy is just put your all into that.”

On the win: “The Knicks ain’t win since what, 19? It be in 2026. Come on, bro. Anybody could do anything.”

An eighteen-year-old on the train says it is his first day going to Manhattan. The reply: “Eighteen. You got time. You got a decade to do everything you want and put your whole self in position. A decade. That’s a lot of time.”

On why he makes content: “You could be from the garbage and still make whatever you want. Don’t think just cause you from whatever, you can do whatever you want in this world.” Filmed in New York, June 2026.
