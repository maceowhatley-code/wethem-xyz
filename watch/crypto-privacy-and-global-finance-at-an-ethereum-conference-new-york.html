Crypto, privacy and global finance at ETHconf: Maggie of Unlink.

Maggie, Reported by Maceo, New York, 13 min · filmed June 2026

To quote this piece: Maggie, “Crypto, privacy and global finance at ETHconf: Maggie of Unlink,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-06, https://wethem.xyz/watch/crypto-privacy-and-global-finance-at-an-ethereum-conference-new-york. Each transcript line links to its second.

The produced piece from the ETH Global week in New York, June 2026, the one the trailer was cut from. It opens on a voice from the floor: “I do believe that New York fundamentally is the financial capital of the world,” then someone at a basketball game toasting “People that love to be decentralized” and a late Shih Tzu. Maceo, in narration: “Well, that’s why I’m here. Let’s find out.”

Then the walk in. “New York doesn’t ease you in. You step out and you’re already in it.” Maceo grew up a couple of hours away, close enough that the city “was never some distant idea.” His statement of purpose is the clearest on tape: “Over the last five years, we’ve been researching how cryptocurrency and decentralized technology can empower everyday people around the world,” and the reason for the trip, to understand “how Ethereum is working to show Wall Street it is ready to be the global settlement layer for financial systems.”

On the floor at ETHconf, Maggie, COO of Unlink, which “builds privacy infrastructure for companies that want to use blockchains privately,” so that money moves “across any EVM blockchain fully privately and compliantly.” A COO on a team of five “takes care of everything that’s not technical in the company,” down to the swag: “We actually ran out of t-shirts.”

Maceo brings Maggie the question people outside asked him that morning, what is crypto? “That’s a really hard question to answer,” Maggie says, then answers it: it “enables anyone in the world to participate in the global financial system,” because the old rails are “very clunky and very slow. and very expensive.” For a 20-year-old, careers, and investing in tokenized assets before you are old enough for a brokerage account. For a 60-year-old, receiving money from family abroad: “your family can essentially now send money to you instantly and also without additional fees.” What is ETH? “the native token on the Ethereum network” and the ecosystem built on it. What sets its builders apart is composability, treating each piece “as a puzzle piece or a building block.”

Maggie’s message for the builders: “get involved,” go to conferences, “Just keep talking to people.” Why New York: “being in New York is a very symbolic way of saying” the traditional rails worked, and now “we’re going to make that even better.” Where Ethereum is: “I think we’re at an inflection point,” and “from here on out, it’s going to be even more obvious that crypto is going to be the center of finance.” Maggie learns the sign-off on camera. The transcript below is from the original audio.
