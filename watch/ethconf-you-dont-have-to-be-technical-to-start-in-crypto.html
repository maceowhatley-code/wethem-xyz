ETHConf: you don't have to be technical to start in crypto.

New York, 5 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Someone who works at Mantle, a layer 2 built on Ethereum, explains the job at ETHConf in New York: “We’re basically trying to be the distribution layer for traditional finance, trying to get them on-chain.” A finance company that wants to come on-chain gets help with the legal and technical side, and then with distribution.

A few years ago, he says, people outside crypto “just thought it was like straight up scams.” He thinks regulation has changed that, and that now is a better time to learn than before. For anyone still nervous: “you don’t really have to be technical, to be honest. I mean, when I started off, I didn’t know anything.”

He treats AI the same way. People worry about jobs and the environment, and he does not wave that away, but “it will hurt you in the long run if you don’t just get on board now.” Because “when the internet came aboard, everyone was like, the internet’s a fad, you know, it’ll go away.” He came to Ethereum from Solana, and calls the move “a natural progression”.
