Two women, a message, and a question back.

Philadelphia, 3 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

To quote this piece: “Two women, a message, and a question back,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/a-question-for-the-city-north-philly-philadelphia. Each transcript line links to its second.

A note before this one: both women here talk about people being killed in their neighbourhood, and about guns, and it stays where they put it.

What is it like being a black woman in Philadelphia? Two women take the camera on together, one from North Philly. “As black people, we need to look out for each other,” the first says. The second: “a lot of killing, a lot of stray bullets going on.” Between them: “Start fighting and put the guns down.”

One says, “It’s tough. It’s hard.” The other: “At the end of the day, we strong black women out here. You can never break a black woman.” On black men: “Some of them are. I’m going to give you your props, but some of y’all gotta do better.” She has skin in it: “I got a son. I’m raising a king.”

Her message to young men: “Don’t let nobody tell you you can’t do shit. You can do whatever the fuck you want to do.” The other woman keeps hers to four words, repeated: “Put the guns down.” Three minutes, Philadelphia.
