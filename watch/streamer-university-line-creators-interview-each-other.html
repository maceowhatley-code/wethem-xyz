Down the line for Kai Cenat’s Streamer University auditions in New York, the people waiting took the microphone and started interviewing each other. A Call of Duty creator names her influences. The longest turn goes to a Brawlhalla player whose plan for Streamer University is a tournament, and who gets roasted when he gives his rank as 1980.
