What does Andrew Callaghan do that other journalists do not? Outside his live show in Denver, fans in the queue answer. Casey found Channel 5 through one episode: “It was professional. It was respectful. He asked the right questions, and he let the man hang himself with his own rope.” His advice to an aspiring journalist: “He keeps his cool for the full hour. He never gets flat.” His thanks: “I like to think that I can do it, but I can’t, and this man does it for me. Thanks for being my avatar.”

Priscilla, a fifth-generation Texan, asks the world: “If you had unlimited resources, what would you create?” Casey asks: “Why aren’t we doing better as a species?”

Longer-term fans who go back to All Gas No Brakes: “He found his niche thing, but he just keeps pulling for the thread.” Denver.
