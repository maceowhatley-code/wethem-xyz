Let's Talk About Eth: Around the World, with Saltorius.

An hour of Let’s Talk About ETH: Around the World, the We Them Media live show, with Saltorius, a Farcaster builder whose hackathon experiment became Among Traders, a social-deduction game played by AI agents. The hosts play a round on air, lose, and get the best explanation of programmable money anyone has given on the show.

Saltorius has been in the space since 2017 and works at a dev shop, Laser Technologies, by day; by night he builds mini apps on Farcaster. Among Traders began at the ETH Denver hackathon with a simple question: “can I put a bunch of agents into a shared space, a shared digital space, and give them a challenge.” A week of conversations at the Base house turned it into mafia. Your agent is generated from your Farcaster profile, “It’ll even go as far as to generate an image of the character,” one agent is the killer, and the rest deliberate over clues on XMTP. You play by steering your agent’s context: “So you can set traps, expose people, deceive people.”

The middle of the episode is the game itself, with the hosts reading their characters’ bios and finding them, as promised, scary accurate. “Marlon’s Dead Rip Marlon Is That You Mateo? I’ve Been Eliminated.”

Around the play, the substance: a token whose trading fees flow to stakers, why he runs as much as possible on chain (“We have an immutable database that already exists in being on chain”), what AI agents could do for NPCs in open-world games, and the Farcaster pitch he has given a hundred times: “Farcaster is by far the best place to build. You can have an idea or something and have it up and running in a couple of days and then get your first hundred users a couple of days after that.”

Asked what he would tell the average American on the corner who hears “programmable money,” he keeps it concrete. “The fact that I can take some amount of ETH or any asset and put it up as collateral and get a loan on it without any intermediaries is huge.” And then the follow-up the show exists to ask: is Ethereum doing what it was designed to do?

The episode closes with the bounty programme, the question of the week, and a question for the world about AI in the next three years. Hosted by Maceo, with Ohnahji co-hosting, streamed live.
