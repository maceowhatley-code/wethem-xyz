I mean, I know it’s hard times.” And in Spanish, for anyone who thinks AI is dangerous: “Muchas veces La gente piensa que el AI es peligroso o te va a quitar un trabajo. Todo lo contrario, te está dando muchas más oportunidades de las que te puedes imaginar.” In English: many people think AI is dangerous or will take a job away, he says. It is exactly the opposite, it hands you far more opportunities than you can imagine.
