A 14-year-old on Philly, the draft, and making it out.

Reported by Maceo, Philadelphia, 5 min

To quote this piece: “A 14-year-old on Philly, the draft, and making it out,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/a-14-year-old-on-philly-the-draft-and-making-it-out-philadelphia. Each transcript line links to its second.

A corner store in Philadelphia, April 2026. He is fourteen, in eighth grade, and plays football. Asked what he thinks about the USA: “I think it’s confusing.” He thinks it will get worse, “and I feel like that’s really going to affect human life and us in general.” Why? The war talk, and something that reached his classroom: “I heard somebody talk to me about the drafts like the other day.” Not his friends. His teacher, “just letting y’all know,” told the class that people eighteen and over could be drafted.

Then the city. “Philadelphia. It’s bad. Like, real bad people get killed every single day.” He describes what he says to people who push him toward the street: “I’m a student, I’m 14 years old in the eighth grade, I got three college offers, I got a lot of high school offers, I got everything, and all I’m trying to do is make it out, so I don’t have to be stuck in this environment.” Walking outside means looking behind you, “Scared that somebody will shoot you or rob you or jump you.” Where does it come from? “it’s a trend.” When he was six or seven he did not see kids his age doing it.

His message to kids under ten: “Keep dreaming. It’s going to happen. Keep going. Keep your education up.” Then, to the point: “You don’t wanna be in the streets, bro.” On football he says the game is “an outlet of anger, sadness, or whatever,” and that a lot of coaches teach a play and expect it by the next day, when “you really gotta teach kids about the whole program of what football can help you with.” The sign of a good coach: “make sure you’re okay at the house,” and he names his own, Coach Nate, who is trying to get him on a tour of Rutgers.

He is a minor, so we do not name him. The transcript below is from the original audio.
