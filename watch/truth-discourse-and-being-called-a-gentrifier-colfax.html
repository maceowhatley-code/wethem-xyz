Gaming culture and street life at the arcade on Colfax.

Reported by Maceo, Denver, 15 min, filmed 8 March 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Answers Is the internet good for us? and How has the place you live changed in your lifetime? and What would you say to someone under ten?, see how other cities answered

Does the internet make the truth easier or harder to reach? Two men outside an arcade on Colfax, from Maryland and Illinois, answer, and then get asked whether they are the gentrifiers.

On truth: “It’s easier to get access to it, but the truth kind of ebbs and flows, where people assume things are the truth and then it gets blasted.” And: “The internet does offer you your truth if you’re looking for it, but it also can offer you the more objective truth. You just have to dig a little deeper and find sources that are actually high quality, not just some Reddit page.”

On discourse: “Nobody can be right. Even if it’s actual facts, there will always be somebody arguing that it’s just not true.”

One has been in Denver ten years and just got married, the other five. They talk about earning above Denver’s minimum wage and still living paycheque to paycheque. Colfax, Denver.
