Housed, then evicted, and what happened to her daughter.

Jenny, Reported by Maceo, Denver, 13 min, filmed 8 March 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

To quote this piece: Jenny, “Housed, then evicted, and what happened to her daughter,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-03-08, https://wethem.xyz/watch/housing-school-and-the-street-from-somebody-born-here-denver. Each transcript line links to its second.

A note before this one: Jenny describes an attempted rape and an assault by her father that put her in hospital. She raised both herself, in a conversation that is mostly about housing and school, and they are on this page because they are her argument.

What has changed in Denver? Jenny, 25, born there in 1999 and on and off the streets since she was thirteen, says: “I think the city has gotten a lot weirder. Let’s put it that way. Not better, not worse.” Then: “It used to be diverse. It used to be unique. And it’s a lot scarier.”

She tells of a man who attacked her at the convention centre at five in the morning with a broken bottle, and who followed her onto the train. What she says is about everyone else: “at least back in the day, like, you know, if something like that happened, there would be somebody there.” And now: “Even if there was somebody there, they wouldn’t have cared.”
