ETHConf: arcade cabinets that onboard you to crypto.

New York · 6 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week →

At ETHConf in Manhattan, June 2026, moments after a player wins a Valley node on his cabinet, Johnny Coughlin, founder and CEO of Valley Arcade Games, explains the idea: “I own an arcade bar and I was looking for a way to get people who come into arcades to onboard onto Web3.” The mechanic: “you go up to this arcade cabinet and you play. If you scan your score, then you earn something from it,” redeemable “for a free drink or a dollar off your pizza or a movie ticket” at the bar, the movie theater or the bowling alley. The nodes running underneath are “effectively a proof of play,” and “effectively an anti-cheat, anti-bot protocol.” Asked what crypto is, he calls himself non-technical and answers simply: “I think of it as digital money.” The design rule behind all of it: “We wanted this to look and feel just like what people are used to. So we’re showing it to them instead of telling them.” For developers, he points to “valley.games, submit your game,” and for players, “start playing. It’s free.” A note on the facts: cash was 14 percent of US consumer payments in 2025 and four in five people had used it in the past month (Federal Reserve); Federal Reserve notes cannot be redeemed for gold or any other commodity (Federal Reserve).

Mentioned in this video: Valley Arcade Games.
