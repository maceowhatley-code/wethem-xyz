Bitcoin as a hedge against a weakening dollar.

Washington, DC, 7 min, filmed 25 September 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Joseph, who goes by Joe, is a junior at Howard University studying sports management, with a minor in political science. He says he’s interested in sports and entertainment law. This conversation happened on Day 1 of the Black Blockchain Summit 2026, on Howard’s campus in Washington, DC.

Joe doesn’t follow crypto closely: “I’m more so into trading like regular ETFs.” He knows Bitcoin’s run: “Bitcoin has had massive growth in the past 10-ish years”. And he traces his understanding of inflation to a specific place: “I learned about inflation through like my economics courses here.”

The talk moves to Bitcoin as a hedge against a weakening dollar, and what losing the dollar’s status as the world’s reserve currency would mean. Joe says it changed how he sees things: “I never thought about it like that.” Asked what he’d ask people everywhere, he turns the question on himself, “What does the value of a dollar mean to you?”, and answers it: “money makes the world go around.”
