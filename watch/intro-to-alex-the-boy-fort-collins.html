A Chicago transplant on Colorado and dorm life.

Alex the Boy, Fort Collins, 6 min, filmed 13 March 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Six minutes on a Colorado street with a group of friends who spend the first minute arguing about which of them counts as local. One says “I was born like 50 miles away. I lived here 3 weeks after I was born, okay? I am a Colorado native.” Another pushes back with a claim of his own: “I’m from Highlands Ranch. I’ve been living in Highlands Ranch my whole life, so I’m a Colorado native.” The one the video is named for stays out of it. “No, I’m from Chicago”, he says, and that is the interview.

His route here was a visit that did not end. “I’m from Chicago. I love Colorado. My sister went here and I came here to visit her, and, uh, I really loved it.” The pitch he gives is unfussy: “weed is legal here. If you like Colorado, if you like weed, if you like something to do throughout your day, come to Colorado because you could go on a fucking hike or whatever.”

Asked what is different from Chicago he keeps it structural rather than sentimental. “being from a city and being from like out here is completely different”, he says, “obviously city life is different than like being out in a college town”, and the town’s advantage is who passes through it, since “you’re pretty close to everybody from like Denver and Boulder”. On the social side he is mixed: creeps at the parties, and enough good people to cover it. His friend’s rule is blunter, stay away from Greek life.

The best answer is the simplest. “Best thing about Colorado? The people. Everybody’s nice here. Everybody understands what it’s like to be another human being”, he says, then “Everybody loves everybody, and everybody should see like that.” It is delivered mid street, with a car going past and somebody in it shouting.

The last third is college. “My sister went to CSU. I came, I came and visited her when I was 16”, and that trip was “The best time. I got to stay in her fucking dorm and everything”, which is where the idea of it landed. Dorm life itself he rates both ways at once: “I think everybody should go at least experience the dorm life”, and “that was probably the worst and best year of my life”. The worst part is arithmetic, “you’re living in like a 10-foot by like 15-foot space with another person”, with a roommate he liked, “I love my roommate. We would smoke up all the time”, and a girlfriend over daily, which he says made it “like living with 2 roommates”. He signs off saying he is not good on camera.
