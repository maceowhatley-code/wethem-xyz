ETHConf: how the daily video is done by 5 PM.

Shanelle, New York, 9 min · filmed June 2026

To quote this piece: Shanelle, “ETHConf: how the daily video is done by 5 PM,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-06, https://wethem.xyz/watch/how-ethglobals-daily-video-is-done-by-5-pm-ethconf-nyc. Each transcript line links to its second.

At the first ETHConf in Manhattan, June 2026, Lucy of Cactus Can is setting up to interview Shanelle, a visual abstract artist who hosts creative workshops at the conference, and the tape opens on a tip Lucy gives her before the questions start: “when you answer my question, try and phrase it in your answer.” Shanelle answers in full sentences, on the value of meeting in person and on New York as “a melting pot of all types of energies, people, experiences, life events.” Then Maceo turns the camera on the crew. Sarah: “we’re a production company from Sydney. We’re called Cactus Can, and we work closely with the ETHGlobal team.” Six people, corporate work at home, and an edit built on the time difference: “we send it back back to Sydney, they can work on it while we’re asleep.” The daily video is done “by 5 PM of each day,” and the one-minute wrap at the end tells the story of “the first ever ETHConf.” Sarah closes on what outsiders still get wrong: “lots of people still think it’s all up in the air and not a real thing.” A note on the facts: 10 percent of US adults used crypto in 2025 (Federal Reserve), and the SEC approved eleven spot bitcoin funds on 10 January 2024, among them the iShares and Fidelity funds (SEC).

Mentioned in this video: CactusCan Media, the production company from Sydney, ETHGlobal.
