Before you come to America: a man from Benin.

Washington, DC, 53 min, filmed 26 September 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

To quote this piece: “Before you come to America: a man from Benin,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-09-26, https://wethem.xyz/watch/before-you-come-to-america-a-man-from-benin-howard. Each transcript line links to its second.

On Day 2 of the Black Blockchain Summit 2026, Maceo stops Silas, from Benin, outside on Howard University’s campus. He grew up in Benin, lived in Nigeria, came to the US “a couple of years ago”, and is not a student: “I’m not a student, I’m hustler.” He talks about music and its vibe, about having no community in DC (“I just move by myself, you know, because too much friend, too much problem”), and about the palace, the place “where community people, they are coming and they talk about the community”. His message to people in Nigeria and Benin is the heart of the tape: “before you’re going to come America, make sure you know yourself,” because “Nobody gonna help you”. He says it again in French and Fon. Asked what he wants to know, he says “how I’m gonna make more money,” and that people everywhere are hungry, “not just like food. They are hungry for spiritual”. Maceo explains inflation and the dollar, and he answers with his own rule: “small, small invest there, small, small invest there, you gonna come to the top where you want”. A note on the facts: Benin uses the West African CFA franc, 577 to the dollar on 24 September 2026 (BCEAO), close to the six hundred he gives; a dollar bought 1,329 naira on 25 September, down from 1,688 in November 2024 (Central Bank of Nigeria); prices rose 3.4 percent in the US and 15.4 percent in Nigeria in the year to August (BLS, Central Bank of Nigeria); the dollar is down 6.9 percent since January 2025 but still 57 percent of world reserves (Federal Reserve, IMF); and the US set up a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve in March 2025 (White House).
