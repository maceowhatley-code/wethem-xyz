On the ground at a Denver protest, talking Project 2025.

Daniel Wheadon, Reported by Maceo, Denver, 21 min, filmed 28 March 2026

To quote this piece: Daniel Wheadon, “On the ground at a Denver protest, talking Project 2025,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-03-28, https://wethem.xyz/watch/after-the-denver-protest-project-2025-in-his-own-words. Each transcript line links to its second.

Why did he march? After a large protest in Denver, a man who calls himself a history nerd and a Christian answers over twenty minutes. This is his account, in his own words.

Daniel Wheadon spells his name for the camera. His summary: “It’s a pretty classic authoritarian coup, to say it very simply. Erosion of the separation of powers, erosion of church and state, erosion of effectively the rule of law.” He does not want to be the boy who cried wolf, and says why he thinks this is the time to use the word.

On Project 2025 and the Heritage Foundation: “For the love of God, people, it’s not a conspiracy. They wrote their playbook.” He says he means it as a Christian: “That is not real Christianity. Christianity doesn’t say demonise your neighbour, lionise the wealthy.”
