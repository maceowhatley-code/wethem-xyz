A traveler on Denver, graffiti and AI.

Jeff, Denver, 5 min, filmed 5 March 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

To quote this piece: Jeff, “A traveler on Denver, graffiti and AI,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-03-05, https://wethem.xyz/watch/a-strippers-take-on-denver-denver. Each transcript line links to its second.

Four and a half minutes on a Denver street with a man who is passing through, filmed with nothing agreed in advance. “We’re just talking about whatever you want to talk about”, the correspondent tells him, and the first thing he picks up is AI.

He is not impressed by it. “I think AI is already being misused”, he says, and what is on offer is not the thing it is sold as: “it’s not real AI anyways. It’s just a giant compendium right now”, siphoning information and placing markers where it is at. Asked who can trust it, he widens the question to “Who can trust anything?” His own test is older than the software: “know yourself before you can know another”, and “How you feel about something, you should know by now if they’re lying to you.”

He has stepped out of the machinery himself. “I haven’t had a phone in about 2 years”, he says. “I’ve been traveling around doing some stuff, checking stuff out. I’m from Montana originally”, and “Grew up in Wyoming and then came from Portland.”

Asked whether he likes it here he starts with a complaint, “Absolutely not. You guys drive like shit, you greenies”, then licenses it, “I’m from Wyoming, so I can say that.” It does not last a breath. “I love Denver. Denver’s an interesting place”, he says, and the case he makes is a landscape one: “It’s got really great trees and plant system going on here”, and “It’s in the middle of like a bowl of mountains but it’s not really the mountains.” The one thing he holds against the city is inherited weather, “you still get that Wyoming blow-off”, from a state where “up there’s like 90-mile-an-hour winds”.

He chooses the next subject himself. “Yeah, let’s talk about the graffiti scene you guys got here.” On the art he is generous and unsentimental in the same sentence: “graffiti is an amazing thing. Life imitates art imitates life”, then “I think some graffiti sucks, but we all know that.” He guesses “I think there might be a couple Banksys here, which is crazy”, and then undercuts it, since he also knows a man selling prints he would not vouch for.

Glass carries the last stretch, and a friend up in Montana who “goes from having a bag of pipes to completely having 2 shops and a limit”. At the sign-off he gives his name as Jeff, gives a handle he warns is adult content, and says what he does for work.
