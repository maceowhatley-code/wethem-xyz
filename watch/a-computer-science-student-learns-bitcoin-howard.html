A computer science student learns Bitcoin.

Washington, DC, 6 min, filmed 25 September 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

To quote this piece: “A computer science student learns Bitcoin,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-09-25, https://wethem.xyz/watch/a-computer-science-student-learns-bitcoin-howard. Each transcript line links to its second.

On Day 1 of the Black Blockchain Summit 2026, at Howard University in Washington, DC, Maceo sits down with Chris, a computer science sophomore from New Jersey. Chris says he does not know much about crypto: “I would say it’s like a source of money.” He asks Maceo how crypto is valued compared to money, and Maceo answers that crypto’s value, like all money, rests on people believing it will hold value in the future: “crypto’s value, it’s like really a casino.” The conversation moves through the gold standard and de-dollarization, and Maceo describes hyperinflation in Nigeria, where he says people can work a hundred hours in a week and still not have enough to feed their kids. Asked what hyperinflation would do to working-class Americans, Chris answers: “it should be even worse, I think, for the middle class, working class, and just everyone.” Maceo tells him, “Don’t let them print you into poverty.” Chris’s closing thought is “to do your research and to know where your money’s going.” The tape ends light, with Chris’s favorite artist and the story of the crypto address that got Maceo into this work.
