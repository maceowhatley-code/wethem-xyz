ETHConf: borrowing against stocks to buy a matcha.

New York, 7 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

At ETHConf in Manhattan, June 2026, Janusz, from Poland and in crypto since 2016, describes where the industry has moved: “right now everything is going into this traditional finance,” after years when “everyone wanted to have like their own blockchain.” His example for what crypto is: “I was in Poland, I was hiring a guy from Philippines, and to pass him the money was so basically hard going over Western Union,” so now “crypto is kind of this super seamless banking layer for cross-world payments.” His own project pairs a card with stock held as collateral: “I pay $5 for a matcha latte, and automatically it borrows against my stock.” “Normally billionaires do this.” The same verification layer, he says, proves you hold a stock without the old paperwork, “you need to go to your broker, take a PDF, send it over by fax email,” and ties it to a perk: “if you own $100 of Netflix stocks for at least 3 months in Castar Tech, in our app, then basically you’ll get 1 month for free of subscription,” while you “can stay liquid.” A note on the facts: ordinary investors can borrow against stock through a margin account, but FINRA’s minimum deposit is $2,000 (SEC); Accor gives its ALL Gold card to holders of one registered share or 50 bearer shares (Accor); the Netflix offer is a deal his team says it is working on.
