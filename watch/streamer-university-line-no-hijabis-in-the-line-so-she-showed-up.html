To quote this piece: “Streamer University line: no hijabis in the line, so she showed up,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-06, https://wethem.xyz/watch/streamer-university-line-no-hijabis-in-the-line-so-she-showed-up. Each transcript line links to its second.

In line for Kai Cenat’s Streamer University auditions in New York, two IRL streamers, one on camera and one behind it, are doing this for their mothers. One of them shared a room with his mom for eighteen years and just got a place of his own, and their editor is a friend who works for free. Then a creator from Ghana who lives in the Bronx looks down the line and counts no hijabis in it. She wore burgundy for Streamer University and Ghana’s colors for home, and if she gets in she wants the girls who wear a hijab to see it.

Filmed in June 2026. The transcript below is from the original audio.
