No Wi-Fi, no AI, and a comic about a bird.

Reported by Maceo, Denver, 8 min, filmed 8 March 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

What is it like to live in Denver with no Wi-Fi and no AI? An illustrator on Colfax moved from Los Angeles three years ago, lives on Capitol Hill and draws everything by hand. “It’s really quiet out here.” Then: “Like dead city, but in a great way.” She misses the ocean. “There’s a pretty good sense of community, especially out here in Cap Hill. I know my neighbors.”

The work: “Everything’s done pen on paper.” On RiNo, where the tech money is, rent is going up and food with it, but that is not her complaint: “I just feel like they’re really boring.”

On AI, as a hand-drawn artist, her answer is no. Asked about gentrification she declines: “I’m probably the worst person ask about that since I just moved here recently.” Eight minutes on Colfax.
