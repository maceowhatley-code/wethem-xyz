A DAO for Black liberation, and the petrodollar.

Milan, Washington, DC, 17 min, filmed 25 September 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

To quote this piece: Milan, “A DAO for Black liberation, and the petrodollar,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-09-25, https://wethem.xyz/watch/dao-for-black-liberation-and-the-petrodollar-howard. Each transcript line links to its second.

Milan came to Day 1 of the Black Blockchain Summit 2026 at Howard University after seeing an ad on Facebook. He is not a Howard student. He is from California, where studying politics led him into blockchain. He calls his focus sovereignty within your community, and has started his own DAO, THP, which he says stands for the healing paradigm. He describes a four-tier, education-based governance structure, a shared treasury and a currency he calls scrip, all enforced by smart contracts rather than trust. Maceo puts Kamal Hubbard’s question to him: is DeFi still a liberatory technology for Black people? Milan answers with his own account of decentralized finance, the 1971 end of the gold standard, the petrodollar, and the GENIUS Act, plus a claim about Vice President JD Vance and de-dollarization, his own reading of a video he saw, not a sourced quote. He closes on real hope, on the show’s closing question, and spells his name for the record.
