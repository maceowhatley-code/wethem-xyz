Red Rocks dreams and AI agents: DJs on a cold night.

Jamar, Reported by Maceo, Denver, 4 min, filmed 5 March 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

To quote this piece: Jamar, “Red Rocks dreams and AI agents: DJs on a cold night,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-03-05, https://wethem.xyz/watch/red-rocks-dreams-and-ai-agents-djs-on-a-cold-night-denver. Each transcript line links to its second.

Outside a Denver show on a cold night in March, a group of DJs and friends on the part of the job that is not the music. One wants a tool that reaches “every promoter. Not just one. Every promoter,” in different states, to spread his merch. Another has played Lightbox before, at Whip Wednesdays, and likes the room: “Lightbox got a great setup, man. I love it over here.”

Where would they want to play? One answer, and everyone agrees with it. “I need to play Red Rocks, that’s my life.” “That’s every DJ’s dream out here in Denver.” The case for AI comes right after, and it is a case for delegation: “you get one of these AI agents and it’ll literally pester everybody.” A person can only do so much, he says, “and you can’t hire someone that’s going to be as competent as this AI fucking computer.” Humans, “they’re going to do something wrong.” The agent “just does it.”

Tomorrow is the Colorado Bassheads’ fifth anniversary after-party at Prism, “it’s going to be our 5th anniversary, the Colorado Baseheads,” and one of them plays it. The venue is new to the others: a glass gallery, “I’m a glass artist. I make, like, pipes, marbles, all kinds of stuff,” that has started hosting after-parties every week. Then an introduction for the record: “I’m Jamar. My DJ name is Dubdronomer. I’m from Denver, Colorado, and I make Dubstep rhythm.”
