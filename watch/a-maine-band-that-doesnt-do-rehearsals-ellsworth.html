A Maine Band That Doesn't Do Rehearsals

Grass Rug, Reported by Maceo, Ellsworth, ME, 28 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

To quote this piece: Grass Rug, “A Maine Band That Doesn't Do Rehearsals,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/a-maine-band-that-doesnt-do-rehearsals-ellsworth. Each transcript line links to its second.

Grass Rug, a jam band from Portland, Maine, on playing a different show every time, before their set at ZAOstock in Ellsworth.

Four members of Grass Rug tell Maceo how they met and why they skip rehearsals to jam in a basement instead. The Grateful Dead and Billy Strings are the vibe they want at their shows. One of them says every show is “like writing a new book.” They are “looking for gigs,” and one says of the festival, “we need more music festivals.” Then Maceo asks about crypto and inflation, and the talk turns to Bitcoin and paying rent. A bit about a betting app and a promo code is a joke; there is no real code.

For the One Question, they each ask their own: “what makes you happy?”, “are you happy?” and what they would do with one week. Then their advice for kids under seven: listen to good music, listen to your parents, never stop playing.
