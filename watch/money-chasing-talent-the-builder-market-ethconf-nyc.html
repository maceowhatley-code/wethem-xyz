ETHConf: in a builder market, money chases talent.

New York, 13 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

At the first ETHConf in Manhattan, June 2026, a builder connected to Kismet Casa and Farcon talks through what a builder market is. He says he is “super excited to be at this Ethereum conference during a builder market because this is where the builders come together,” and “more excited than I have been in probably 4 years.” His definition: “I would define a builder market by money chasing talent instead of money.” The example he gives is an art auction at Farcon, Farcaster’s conference in Rome, where work made during Kismet Casa was sold through a mini app: “The artist automatically received the money from the winning bid, and then the art was exchanged right there in person.” On the Ethereum Foundation: “whether you agree Ethereum Foundation is doing their job or not doesn’t matter because Ethereum is still doing its job every single day.” Asked what nobody is working hard enough to solve: “Any people problems. I mean, that is the current issue, right? It’s like developers don’t know how to cater to people.” His line on who should be building: “You don’t have to be the smartest, you don’t have to be the best, You don’t have to be the most connected. You don’t have to have the most resources. You do need to be the most passionate.” A note on the facts: on Coinbase, ether rose about 560 percent during 2021, from $731 to a $4,806 peak, and about 4,200 percent from its March 2020 low, short of the 10,000 percent he guesses (Coinbase prices via FRED).

Mentioned in this video: Kismet Casa, the residency for onchain artists and developers, FarCon, the Farcaster conference.
