You teach the kids to play the game right: G, 57.

G, Reported by Maceo, Philadelphia, 8 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

Why did you never speak to the umpires? Maceo stops an umpire on a North Philadelphia ball field because he played baseball himself: “I never took time to really speak to the umpires, but you guys make all the calls, right?”

G is 57, from North Philly, has umpired for ten years, and played all his life: “92, 93, 94, 95, four years in a row.” What makes a championship team: “You gotta have love for the game. Love for the game and play the game right.” What coaches should not do: “Yell at the kids. Let the kids be kids. Let them have fun.”

On the job: “You teach the kids and that’s what I’m here to do. Teach the kids to play the game right.” On what the community can do: “Support the kids. Come out. Cheer for the kids. Cheer for your kids. Cheer for other kids.”

Seven minutes, North Philadelphia, April 2026.
