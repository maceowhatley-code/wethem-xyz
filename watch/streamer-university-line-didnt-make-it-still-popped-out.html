The camera finds people still coming off the audition line for Kai Cenat’s Streamer University in New York, now that the rain has stopped. The sun is back out. The first message for Kai Cenat is not about streaming at all: “the way you’re chasing this, chase God the same way, bro.” The man beside him started six months ago: “I just started and I’m already at 1,100 followers, bro.”

A creator from Rhode Island did not get in and came anyway, from four or five hours away. “I was, but I didn’t make it.” Her reasoning: “You never know who’s watching. So I’m popping out.” She has no crew: “One woman machine. No cameraman, no nothing.” And her message for Kai: “I did not come here to apply. I came here to graduate.”

A man who never joined the line describes the crush at the door and shrugs it off: “Me personally, I came out here fully knowing I’m probably not gonna make it, but you know, content is content.” Near the end, a woman who got inside, too late to be seen, and who made a three-part film asks Kai for one thing: “Look at my film, look at my, my video.” And: “I just hope he sees it.”
