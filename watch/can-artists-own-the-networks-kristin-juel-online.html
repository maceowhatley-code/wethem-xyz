An artist manager on fans, touches and wallets.

Kristin Juel, Reported by Maceo, Online, 51 min

To quote this piece: Kristin Juel, “An artist manager on fans, touches and wallets,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/can-artists-own-the-networks-kristin-juel-online. Each transcript line links to its second.

Can artists own the networks? Maceo asks Kristin Juel, an artist manager of ten years in Los Angeles, on a fifty-one minute call. They worked together the previous autumn on Web3 fan journey research for Water and Music, and this is the follow up: how an audience gets built, and who owns it.

She set up her practice in 2013 “after leaving a 25-year career in advertising and brand strategy”, to “focus on helping to guide artists to know their worth.” On the work: “my work isn’t just management, my work is futurist.” One artist she helped take back the masters of a debut album, “a big learning on what it means to regain your voice, to regain your truth.”

On mystery: “I do believe there should be a sense of mystery about some things.” And the rule from advertising: “it takes like 22 to 27 different touches that they hear the word.”
