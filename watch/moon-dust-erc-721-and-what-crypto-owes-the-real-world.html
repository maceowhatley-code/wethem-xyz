William Entriken, lead author of ERC-721.

Will, Reported by Maceo, Philadelphia, 12 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

To quote this piece: Will, “William Entriken, lead author of ERC-721,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/moon-dust-erc-721-and-what-crypto-owes-the-real-world. Each transcript line links to its second.

Why does the lead author of ERC-721 say the most valuable things crypto has produced are not financial? Will, 41, from Philly, is lead author of the standard every NFT is built on, and he is careful with credit: “Dieter Shirley is the creator. He is the second author, and we have two others in the community too.”

On Ethereum: “I just see it as Vitalik’s creation and I’d like to see him let it go. But the community around Ethereum is larger than that.” On where NFTs fit: “If Bitcoin is the challenge to fiat money, then Ethereum would be the challenge to centralised control of value. But NFT is really the connection to specific individual things outside of the nerd space.” His own work has been in agriculture and royalty payments.

On Argentina: “If your grandma was in Argentina, she would know about Bitcoin already.” People told him the peso only goes down. “At least Bitcoin goes up sometimes.”

A public good, he says, is “Anything that if it exists it can serve everybody.” Twelve minutes at a pizza party in Philadelphia.
