Streamer University line: is streaming the new version of news?

New York · 5 min · filmed June 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week →

A young YouTuber in line for Kai Cenat’s Streamer University auditions in New York is quiet because he is saving his energy for the room. He stopped making videos after middle school and came back to it as an adult. Asked about Kai Cenat, he gives the answer the interviewer calls excellent. When a streamer asks chat how they feel about something in the news, that is news now, and entertainment too, and Kai set the mark early. Then three friends from Asbury Park, one six months into making content and one who has filmed herself since she got a phone, on Kai keeping young Black men occupied and out of trouble.
