Someone who was around for the Justin.tv days is standing in line outside Kai Cenat’s Streamer University auditions in New York. Back then streaming was a hobby and nobody expected it to get big. Some people joined this line at three in the morning, and the line itself is the proof. After that comes a rapper who offers to run the cafeteria and calls Kai Cenat the Michael Jackson of streamers, and an artist who streams every day and says nobody grinds harder.
