Know your rights, and don’t get played.

Reported by Maceo, Denver, 8 min

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

To quote this piece: “Know your rights, and don’t get played,” We Them Media, https://wethem.xyz/watch/know-your-rights-and-dont-get-played-denver. Each transcript line links to its second.

What question should Denver be answering? A man from Las Vegas, in Denver for one night, goes through AI, leadership and the thing he keeps returning to: that most of what happens to people happens because they did not know their rights.

On AI: “you can’t trust it, you don’t know if it’s real, if that’s really your girlfriend.” It is a tool for getting started, he says, not for thinking.

On leadership: “our society just right now is just really a lack of leadership.” He dates the last time it felt otherwise to around 2004, when he was in high school.

On Trump, he says people believed in him because they wanted change, and that the attention went to the wrong place: “he just thinks about the Mexican border.” Maceo explains crypto to him in the middle of the conversation.

Denver, 2026.
