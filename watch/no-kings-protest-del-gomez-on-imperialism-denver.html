No Kings: Del Gomez on empire.

Del Gomez, Reported by Maceo, Denver, 6 min, filmed 28 March 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week

To quote this piece: Del Gomez, “No Kings: Del Gomez on empire,” We Them Media, filmed 2026-03-28, https://wethem.xyz/watch/no-kings-protest-del-gomez-on-imperialism-denver. Each transcript line links to its second.

Five and a half minutes at the No Kings protest in Denver with a man who gives his name and spells it for the camera: “my name is Del Gomez. That’s D-E-L-G-O-M-E-Z.”

Asked to explain the day for anyone who does not know what it is, he does: “today is a protest called No Kings. It’s kind of like a worldwide protest, actually, that’s protesting Trump, who claims to be the king of this, the democratic, prime country of the world.”

What follows is his argument, and the piece publishes it as his rather than as ours. He frames it as a question of empire rather than of one man, and is careful to separate the country from the people running it, “not me, but those people, the people that have hijacked our government”.

The opening thirty seconds are kept in as well, because they are the job: a camera being set up, a subject asking “just cut out the vape, okay, please do that for me”, and an interviewer admitting he is winging the shot. The request was honoured in the edit.

This is one voice at one protest, recorded because he was willing to give it. It is not the channel’s position and it is not presented as the only one.
