Durgadas, on coordinating people and capital.

Over the last two years, Durgadas has been part of the We Them Media community. We originally connected through 40 Acres DAO, a Black-centered organization building in crypto. At ETHConf in Manhattan, we sit down with him to dive deep into the work he has been doing on organizational coordination and how to actually prevent the failure patterns we keep seeing in DAOs and crypto communities.

How do organizations actually coordinate people and capital efficiently? “People start off with this intent that they want to make something which is different and then use exactly the same structural language and approaches and everything that constructed an unequal system to begin with,” Durgadas explains. The solution? Alignment between intention and structure, something most organizations completely miss.

Durgadas is a standards architect at Octant working on public goods grants funding. Over the last six months, he has created 15 new standards specifically designed to prevent organizational failure, his “Coordination Structural Integrity Suite,” now open-source on GitHub. He is also building “proof of coordination” as the next evolution beyond traditional governance models.

His work draws on indigenous concepts of role-based obligation, duties in six directions rather than top-down authority, consciousness studies, and his own experience witnessing why communities like Gravity DAO fail. He is writing a book on decolonization inspired by James Baldwin, grounded in lived experience working with Native American communities for eight years and unpacking intergenerational trauma.

The conversation ranges from what crypto actually means as a social justice movement, to neuroscience and consciousness, to why the most welcoming communities he has found have been in web3, a perspective he brings as an autistic builder who worked at Microsoft, Apple and Cisco before finding his real home here.
