Where the homeless went before the convention came.

John · Denver · 18 min · filmed 30 March 2026

In this video? Ask for a change or have it taken down.

Keep up with new releases. Get the newsletter, once a week →

A note before this one: near the end John talks about two people who were shot, and about the videos of it. He raises it himself, in the middle of an argument about deportation, and it sits here where he put it.

What did people in a conservative white town say out loud in the civil rights era? John, who has lived in Denver a long time, says the words were the quiet part: “they would work around it. But they would still subject people to like not being able to do what they should be able to do.” And: “And they kept it quiet.” Asked the motive: “Because they’re racist.” He is unhappy to say it: “I hate to say that cuz I want people to be better than that.”

He calls it arithmetic: “Within 20 25 years, Hispanics will hold the majority of the people in our country.” And: “it’s ironic because we stole the United States in the first place.”

Seventeen minutes on a Denver street.
