The Arc
Season 01, 2026

The arc from launch to completion.

Season 01 is a single six-month arc with two real tentpoles: June 1 launch (done) and November 30 completion. From September 15 to October 12 a new piece goes public on YouTube every day, starting with the Streamer University line from New York. December is rest. Whatever Season 02 looks like opens after a deliberate pause.

  • Now, Sep 2026 A new piece on YouTube every day from 15 September to 12 October, starting with the Streamer University line. The We Them Weekly newsletter launched. One Question running: 150 interviews by 4 November, 61 in so far. 8 monthly supporters at $178 a month through Fractured Atlas. Long form now dropping.
  • Spring 2026, pre-launch Practice period. Bounties opened, contributors shooting, applications out to fiscal sponsors. Website and standards public. Editorial guidelines drafted with collaborators.
  • May 2026 Standards published, now folded into the Mission page. Tiny News Collective application submitted. Bitcoin Pizza Day bounty run and paid out.
  • Jun 1, 2026 Network went live. The full publishing stack ran end-to-end for the first time.
  • Jun → Aug 2026 The model tested under real conditions in real cities: Lagos, Abuja, Nairobi, Philadelphia, Charlotte, plus Marissa’s rolling state-by-state beat. Biggest push of the summer was the ETH Conf rollout.
  • Q3 2026 A pilot on one story, recording its research step by step from first question to publication. Talked to CTRL+X about licensing a few pieces.
  • Q4 2026 A public review of Season 01, covering what worked and what we would change. Twelve strongest contributors selected for retainer slots as lead voices for their cities.
  • Nov 30, 2026 Season 01 closes. Completion conversation with the team, recorded, public, named acknowledgments for everyone who showed up across the season. December is rest. Season 02 plans open in January 2027.

The Mission page walks the same arc in more depth, with the publishing stack and the rules for what gets shipped.
