For advisors, partners and funds

What this is, how it runs, and what we are asking for.

Everything on this page comes from the record or from a dated file, not from a pitch deck. The numbers are counted from the archive. The chart is drawn from the channel’s real publish dates. If a figure here disagrees with a figure anywhere else on the site, the site is wrong and this page is right, and I want to know.

The model, in two sentences.

Editorial: We Them Media

Street journalism and builder interviews from 16 countries, published free with the transcript and no advertising. Paid for by monthly supporters through Fractured Atlas (our fiscal sponsor) and by bounties that fund specific stories.

Commercial: We Them Worldwide LLC

Commissioned production for brands and events, executed with SuperBio, our production partner in Abuja. Same network, a different product, and a separation we enforce: funding does not direct the editorial work, and the standards say so in public.

Cadence.

Public videos published on the channel per month, the last eighteen months. 375 in the last 90 days, 804 in the last twelve months. The spring of 2026 is the Denver and Philadelphia street work. The summer is ETH Global, the Channel 5 carnival, and the first contributors filing from their own cities.

Who has paid for this so far, on tape.

No institution has funded We Them Media yet. Everything below is a person, a DAO vote, or a bounty, and each one is said out loud in a piece on this site, quoted from the transcript and linked to the second. The pattern is the pitch: small money, put up by people who wanted a specific thing filmed, and the thing got filmed.

What the money is for.

Wages first: contributors are paid for the work, and the goal on tape is a paid contributor in every country where we have a supporter. Then the archive: transcription, the pages, the open dataset. Then reach: the Weekly Connect, the newsletter, and the season releases. The supporter page shows the running total and what each level covers, State of the network has the quarter.

The ask.

Advisors: two or three people who have run a newsroom, a community institution, or a media business, for a monthly hour and honest notes. Partners: institutions in the cities we film in who want their community documented on its own terms. Funds: the conversation about what a community-owned archive is worth when the footage is the product and the people in it are the owners. Write to wethem.xyz@gmail.com, I answer it myself.
