Is the internet good for us?

Length 2:54 Length 1:25 Length 4:43 Length 14:38

Asked four different ways in the same fortnight, which is itself part of the record: has the internet made it easier or harder to get information; is it better at spreading information or misinformation; is it making the truth easier or harder to reach; is it good for us. Nairobi and Denver, a bounty question put by three different contributors.

Almost nobody said yes or no. The common answer, in both cities, was that the internet is exactly as good as what you go looking for, and that the work has moved from finding things to telling them apart.

Who answered, and what did they say?

These are the speakers’ own words, as they said them. We have not checked their claims.

    “I think the internet has made it easier, but confusing for people to get access to information.”

  2. Length 1:25

    Both. It is not one-sided.

    “I think it’s both. It’s not one-sided.”

    A tool for both. Be discerning.

    “I think it’s both. It’s definitely a tool for both. … you kind of need to be discerning, you know, be like a critical thinker with your sources”

  4. Length 14:38

    Easier to reach; the truth ebbs and flows.

    “the truth kind of ebbs and flows in a way where people assume things are the truth and then it kind of gets blasted”

    Chase and James, on Colfax, Denver · The full piece · at 0:48

  5. Length 12:15

    Absolutely good for us, and it makes your opinions crazier.

    “The internet is absolutely good for us. But I do see the downsides. … when you end up in an environment where you never hear anything but your own opinion, your opinion gets more and more crazy”

    Jake and Shannon, on East Colfax, Denver · The full piece · at 4:55

  6. Length 5:53

    A good thing, with some bad things to it.

    “I think the internet is a good thing. It has some bad things to it, too.”

    The Clearinghouse Bandit, four months out, Denver · The full piece · at 4:40

  7. Length 1:27

    The truth is a subjective format; piece out what is consistent.

    “It’s interesting because the truth is a subjective format. The truth is anything that is set forth by a person. You can tell me your truth, but your truth is not my truth.”

    A voice from Nigeria, for the bounty board · The full piece · at 0:00

  8. Length 1:28

    A tool for both: scammers in every corner, and the people who debunk them.

    “I would honestly think that it’s a tool for both. There’s scammers on all parts of the internet, every little nook and cranny and corner, right?”

    A second voice from Nigeria · The full piece · at 0:11

  9. Length 1:39

    A double-edged sword, depending on your due diligence.

    “I think the internet is a double-edged sword. So on one end, it’s made it a lot easier for you to access factual information.”

    A voice from Kenya · The full piece · at 0:00

  10. Length 6:39

    Good, and always evolving.

    “It’s good. It’s an ever-changing thing, and it’ll always be a great thing that’s evolving.”
