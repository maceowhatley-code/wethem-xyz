What do you think of the United States?

Length 2:56 Length 5:00 Length 5:00 Length 2:13

Put to people in Abuja and Lagos by two Nigerian contributors, and, from the inside, to a young man in Charlotte after a pick-up soccer game. The Nigerian answers come with their sources attached, which is what makes them useful: nobody claims to have been there, and everybody says where the picture came from.

The answer from inside the country is the one that agrees with the answers from outside it: the dream is real, and it is for somebody else.

Who answered, and what did they say?

These are the speakers’ own words, as they said them. We have not checked their claims.

  1. Length 2:56

    A very big state that controls the world’s affairs.

    “we know US to be a very big big state. And they control most of the affairs in the world.”

    Paul, in Abuja · The full piece · at 0:52

  2. Length 5:00

    Racism. School shootings.

    “Racism. … School shooting.”

    First answers on the street, Lagos · The full piece · at 0:15

  3. Length 5:00

    A nice place, but Nigeria is the best.

    “I would say it’s a nice place, but I don’t have any opinion yet because Nigeria is the best for me.”

    An artist, Lagos · The full piece · at 2:39

  4. Length 2:13

    The dream is here, and it is not for everybody.

    “it’s here, but it’s not for everybody … common folk, not so much”

    After a soccer game, Charlotte · The full piece · at 0:29

  5. Length 9:11

    The majority of people respect the law.

    “I like this country because all the... All the people, or the majority of the people Respect the law”

    Elios, from Venezuela, Denver · The full piece · at 1:55

  6. Length 6:36

    Here you leave work and pass a supermarket.

    “Aquí tú sales del trabajo Y pasas por un supermercado Y si quieres comprarle el supermercado completo Lo compras”

    Romani, from Cuba, in Denver · The full piece · at 6:09

  7. Length 28:08

    More opportunity here than in Canada.

    “I just feel like there’s more opportunity in the U.S. than there is Canada.”

    A Canadian in Times Square, New York · The full piece · at 3:39

  8. Length 2:41

    The draft would take other pathways away.

    “if you want to go to the military, you should go to the military, but other people, they got other careers or pathways they want to do, so that’s just taking away from other people”

    Jake, in Philadelphia · The full piece · at 0:38

  9. Length 2:41

    Prices up, nothing given back.

    “he’s upping prices on everything, I feel as though that’s bad just because people that don’t really have it or are struggling, he’s making it more worse for people to live”

    Tay, in Philadelphia · The full piece · at 1:18

  10. Length 5:07

    Confusing, and going to get worse.

    “I think it’s confusing. Because, like, it’s like, they got so much stuff going on.”

    A 14-year-old in a Philadelphia corner store · The full piece · at 0:12

  11. Length 3:46

    Hates it: the government, healthcare, and no funding for education.

    “I hate it. Okay. Sucks. Government sucks. Healthcare. Like, there’s a lot of things.”

    Danielle, 25, Philadelphia · The full piece · at 0:12

  12. Length 10:09

    He went back to Finland to see if he wanted to stay, and came home.

    “here I was more free. I didn’t need to belong to anyone. I didn’t need to listen to the same thing every day.”

    A Finnish immigrant in Vermont · The full piece · at 1:03

  13. Length 1:11:44

    What it does to people his age, sent to the military.

    “I feel like it’s bad. Like what Trump I guess what he doing to people like our age I guess like went to the military or something like that. I feel like that’s not good aspect for us.”

    Jake, on Let’s Talk About Eth · Watch on YouTube · at 43:53
