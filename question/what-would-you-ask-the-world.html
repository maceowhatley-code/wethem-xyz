If you could ask people around the world one question, what would you ask them?

Length 1:47 Length 16:28 Length 20:14 Length 8:11

This is the question the whole programme is built on. It has been put, on camera, to people in Denver, Lagos, New York and Philadelphia, and each of these is what one person said when asked what they wanted to know from everybody else.

They are the bank the weekly question is drawn from. A question collected on a street in Lagos can be put to somebody in Philadelphia with the Lagos answer already sitting next to it, and every one below is credited to the person who asked it, with a link to the moment they did. Two answers that were jokes rather than questions are left on the tape; everything anybody sincerely asked is here.
