Kamal Hubbard.

Research lead at We Them Media, host of Crypto with Kamal · author of DeFi for the Diaspora

Kamal is a lawyer by training, certified in fraud examination, cybersecurity and decentralized finance. He founded CageChain Media Group, which publishes his book, and he hosts Crypto with Kamal, a show of conversations with people working in crypto.

He testified before the California Senate Banking Committee on the basics of blockchain and Bitcoin. With the Blockchain Advocacy Coalition he backed two California bills, AB 2658 and SB 838, which gave blockchain a definition in state law and let businesses transfer equity on a distributed ledger.

At We Them Media he runs the research behind our coverage and co-writes The We Them Weekly, our newsletter.

Kamal's book

DeFi for the Diaspora

The history of money, where it has left Black communities around the world, and how decentralized finance could be used to change that.
