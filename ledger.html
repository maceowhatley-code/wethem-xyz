What we have not published.

Every archive has this list and almost nobody prints it. These reasons are not written for this page. They are read straight out of the files where the decisions were made, so this page cannot flatter them. If a reason reads badly, the reason is bad.

  • Crypto as a Social Justice Movement, and a Place to Belong
