A New Type of Media Organization for a New World.

We Them Media is a new type of media organization for a new world. We are citizen journalists: over 700 creators, technologists and leaders across the African Diaspora and the globe, asking real questions on our own streets in 16 countries. What is crypto? What is happening to our money? Learn about crypto with us, explore how the journey led us here, and join the community doing the asking.

Every project is on one page, the portfolio, among them the Black Blockchain Summit, Streamer University, ETH Global, Pizza Day, One Question, Nigeria, Kenya, the Channel 5 Carnival, the Denver year, the Colorado DJ and music scene, the Global Majority Hub in Bangkok, the single dispatches and Let’s Talk About ETH: Around The World.

The rest of this page is the fastest honest tour of it: three pieces to watch, how it is made and paid for, the numbers as the archive counts them, and what happens next. If you have four minutes, this is the page. If you have one, watch Mr. P.

How it is made, and paid for.

Contributors film where they live and are credited by name; the people page lists who filmed what. Editorial takes no advertising and runs no paywall. It is paid for by monthly supporters through Fractured Atlas, our fiscal sponsor, and by bounties that fund specific stories. Commercial work runs through a separate company, We Them Worldwide LLC, with production by SuperBio in Abuja: same network, two products, and a separation we enforce rather than describe. The seven rules we publish and are held to, starting with me, are on the standards page.

What happens next.

Season 01 opened June 1, 2026 and runs to November 30. The goal is the one I said on tape in April, scaled: a contributor in every country where we have a supporter, paid for the work, with the archive kept on the open web where anyone can check a claim against a face. The channel holds 791 videos and this site has written up 291; the rest is being transcribed and read now. Every Wednesday at noon New York time the Weekly Connect is open to anyone: we watch street interviews together and go over what the team is working on.
