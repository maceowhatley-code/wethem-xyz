Editorial side. We Them Media is a community-driven media platform, with work from 16 countries. Field footage and on-the-ground coverage. Season 01 opened June 1, 2026 and runs through November 30.

Commercial side. We Them Worldwide LLC contracts with brand clients. SuperBio, our production partner in Abuja, Nigeria (Bayo, Musah, Yama), handles production execution on B2B engagements.

Editorial stays independent and commercial pays the bills. Same network, two different products, and a separation we enforce rather than describe.

Revenue stage: pre-profit. Primary mix is commissioned production, monthly supporter donations via Fractured Atlas, and selective angel backing.

The ETH Conf rollout. Conference coverage went out across the channel as part of Season 01, which opened June 1.

#QueryTheWorld kicked off. One question, put to people on their own streets across the network, answered on camera. See how it works.

We started fundraising. Recurring support went from zero to eight people at $178 a month, and The We Them Weekly launched as our newsletter with Kamal running research and co-authoring it.

More than thirty shorts went out between June and August, with long form now in rotation alongside them. We now post seven days a week across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and X. This is the stretch where publishing on a schedule stopped being an intention and became a habit.

What that adds up to. The We Them Media catalog and archive are public on this site, and every transcript is searchable. The next project starts from that instead of from zero. That is the bet we are making.

I am proud for the world to see what I have spent these last few years doing.

– Maceo

04, Capital
What we are raising

What we are raising.

We are not raising a priced round at this time. Inbound investor conversations are welcome and we respond.

The current capital strategy is a mix of commissioned production revenue, monthly supporter donations via Fractured Atlas, and selective angel backing as the cooperative legal structure formalizes.

For investor inquiries, call Maceo directly, or DM any of our social pages and we will get you to him.

05, Honest about uncertainty
What is not yet decided

What we are still figuring out.

Distribution strategy across YouTube, the newsletter, and partner channels is still forming. We have a thesis. We do not yet have evidence.

The cooperative legal structure is being formalized. Counsel is engaged. The timeline is months, not weeks.

Pizza Day in May 2026 was a pilot for community activation. We are still learning what worked and what to repeat.

The flagship show partnership is in negotiation. It is not signed.
