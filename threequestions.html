We Them Media x poidh #QueryTheWorld Worldwide 25 Sep–28 Oct 2026

A collective inquiry. Ask about inflation, ask the One Question, and bring back the answers.

Pot: $5 · pot open

Inflation, and the One Question. Any camera, any city. If you have been in the crypto and builder space and you have something to say about how inflation touches your community, this is the bounty for that. Submissions open now.

01 · The questions
On camera, in this order

The questions. Inflation first, then the One Question.

Question 01 · first
"What do you know about inflation?"
Question 02 · always
"If you could ask people around the world one question, what would you ask them?"
The effort

That second one is the same question we have been asking since the start. Do not shorten it. Do not paraphrase it. Ask it exactly like that, and let them answer it themselves. If the person leaves before you get to it, the submission still counts, but we want both so that future initiatives can reflect what people are asking. After both, riff: follow up on what they say, mix in your own questions, share your own thoughts. The two questions come first; everything after is yours.

02 · How to enter
Six steps, all on camera

How to enter. Six steps, start to finish.

1
Start your video by saying: "This is for poidh and We Them Media. I am [name] in [city, country]." Then spell your name, and spell the place if it might be new to somebody.
2
Ask "What do you know about inflation?" on camera. Then ask the One Question.
3
Before you stop rolling, ask their name and their handle on camera. Ask them to spell it. Ask if it is okay to post it. We are going to tag whatever they tell us.
4
Before you wrap, ask them on camera to follow We Them Media on Instagram (@we_them_media) and subscribe on YouTube (@WeThemMediaHQ). They do not have to say yes. You do have to ask, on camera, for the entry to count.
5
End the video yourself, on camera, with the call: invite whoever is watching to help fund We Them Media, your citizen journalism news network, so we can query the world, at wethem.xyz/supporter. Your own words are fine. Without the call, the entry does not count.
6
Upload it to your own YouTube channel in the format below, then post the YouTube link on Farcaster in the /wethemmedia channel and tag @poidhxyz and @wethemmedia. We play it from there. If it is not in the channel, it does not count. On Instagram, add @we_them_media as a collaborator on your post if you want to grow there too.

Format. One interview or a compilation, any length. Your phone is fine. If the interaction appears scripted, staged, or rehearsed, the entry will not count.

On YouTube. Title: What Do You Know About Inflation? | [city, country]. First line of the description: Filmed for the One Question bounty from We Them Media and poidh: wethem.xyz/threequestions. Then who filmed it and who is in it, with names and handles as they gave them, and the date. Set it to Public, leave “Allow embedding” on (YouTube Studio, the video’s details, Show more, License and distribution), and choose “No, it’s not made for kids”. If we cannot play it from the link, it cannot be judged.

03 · What we are looking for
Stellar is a service, not a length

What counts as stellar. A service to the people watching.

Stellar is not a length or a look. It is whether the answer does a service to your community: something the people where you live would actually want to hear. If a stranger where you are from would stop scrolling and say "that is what I think too" or "I never thought about it that way," you got it. Videos that demonstrate a commitment to being informative and entertaining will be treated as exemplary. We want work that makes people want to watch and then go talk about what they saw.

04 · The pot
Starts at $5, grows in public

$5, and it grows from here. Every entry is one ticket.

  • $5. This is a bounty for those who want to play big, and we thank you for dreaming big with us this season.
  • Anyone can add to the pot, and the page shows it as it grows. The better the submissions, the more people will want to raise the pot and back this inquiry with their own money.
  • How we pick: every eligible entry is one ticket. We raffle the pot among them.
  • Campaign goal: 150 interviews. That is the number we are trying to hit.
  • Stretch: 300 interviews. If we hit 300, We Them Media adds $300 to the pot, split across 30 people at $10 each.
  • Closes Wednesday, October 28, 2026.
  • As many entries as you like, one per person per week.

06 · Credits and your channel
Your name goes on the work

Your name goes on it. Win or not.

Every piece we publish names who filmed it, and where. That is not optional and it is not small print. If you go out and get a stellar answer, your name goes on it.

Every eligible submission goes into the archive at wethem.xyz, indexed and searchable, with your name on it as the person who filmed it, and where. Win or not, the work stays up under your credit.

All videos will be up on YouTube and surfaced on our website. If you post yours on YouTube and link to it, we will use that to add to our site and funnel viewers to your channel.

Every qualifying submission is being counted as a contribution to our One Question initiative.

07 · Participation
Showing up now counts later

Showing up now counts later. This is how we build it.

Participation in all bounties is tracked. The people who show up and do the work get counted when new programs open. Here is what that means: new raffles will be able to count past participation in participation-gated raffles. If you show up now, you will be retroactively allocated in a future raffle on top of this season's bounty. This is how we build it.
