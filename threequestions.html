Teach someone. Show they learned it, and bring back their One Question.

Pot: open, split evenly at the end

Teach one person how bitcoin, inflation and fiat money relate, in one continuous shot, then show on camera that they learned it. Our goal is for our content to cumulatively teach 100,000 people around the world how money, inflation and bitcoin work. It is what brought us together, so let’s talk about it. We split the whole pot evenly between all qualifying posts at the end.

01, The questions
On camera, in this order

On camera, in this order. Nine short steps.

You teach one person. They say it back in their own words, and they give us their One Question for the world and how they would answer it. We cut the questions and answers into Shorts, so the moments that are most engaging do best. Have fun.

The before
"What is inflation, and what do you think bitcoin has to do with it?"
The three check questions
"What does inflation do to the money you have?", "Why can more fiat money be made, but not more bitcoin?", "What would you want to know before you trusted bitcoin to protect your money?"
The One Question, always
"If you could ask people around the world one question, what would you ask?" Then: "How would you answer it?"
What to teach

What inflation is. What fiat money is and why governments can make more of it. What bitcoin is, including its fixed supply of 21 million and what that does and does not do about inflation. Say plainly that bitcoin’s price swings a lot and that people disagree on whether it protects anyone’s money. Teach the idea and leave out investment advice and price predictions.

02, How to enter
On camera, then three steps to enter

How to enter. Film it, post it, claim it.

1
Say: "I'm [name] and I'm reporting with We Them Media and poidh from [place] on [date]." Spell your name.
2
Introduce your guest by asking: "What's your name, how old are you, and where are you from?" Friends are fine.
3
Ask the before, teach, then ask them to explain it back in their own words and answer the three questions.
4
Ask for their One Question for the world, and how they would answer it. Then have them say: "I'm [name] and you can post this."
5
Spell your social handles, Instagram first, or put them in your Farcaster post. We use them to post your video, tag you and add you as a collaborator on Instagram.
6
End with: "If you enjoyed this video or learned something, go to wethem.xyz to support more work like this. Thank you for watching!"
7
Upload it to your own YouTube channel in the format below.
8
Post the YouTube link on Farcaster in the /wethemmedia channel and tag @wethemmedia and @poidhxyz.
9
Submit a claim on the poidh bounty with a screenshot of your Farcaster post, the link to it, and your favorite thing about the video in a sentence or two. We check the link. The screenshot is the record.

Format. One continuous shot, no cuts, no speed changes, no stitching. Your phone is fine. A real person has to be learning: no scripts, no actors, no coaching off camera, no AI. A different guest in each video. A child on camera needs a parent or guardian’s OK on tape, and we never publish a child’s name, handle or location.

On YouTube. Title: Teaching [name] Bitcoin and Inflation | [city, country]. First line of the description: Filmed for the Teach It bounty from We Them Media and poidh: wethem.xyz/onequestion. Then who filmed it and who is in it, with names and handles as they gave them, and the date. Set it to Public, leave “Allow embedding” on, and choose “No, it’s not made for kids”. If we cannot play it from the link, we cannot judge it.

See what it looks like. A computer science student learns Bitcoin, at Howard University.

03, What we are looking for
Stellar is a service, not a length

What counts as stellar. A service to the people watching.

Stellar depends on whether the answer does a service to your community: something the people where you live would actually want to hear. If a stranger where you are from would stop scrolling and say "that is what I think too" or "I never thought about it that way," you got it. We treat videos that demonstrate a commitment to being informative and entertaining as exemplary. We want work that makes people want to watch and then go talk about what they saw.

04, The pot
Split evenly at the end, grows in public

The pot. Split evenly between all qualifying posts.

  • It is not given to the best. At the end, we split the whole pot evenly between all qualifying posts.
  • We expect other contributions. Anyone can add to the pot on poidh, and it grows as more people teach.
  • Only 5 of your videos count toward the money. More still go in the archive.
  • Payouts go out by hand after poidh’s 2.5% and network fees, each with its transaction posted.
  • Bonus awards. All through the campaign, Maceo may add to the pot or give extra awards to videos that have passed. Sending a good one early gives it more chances.
  • Closes Thursday, December 10, 2026 at 11:59 pm PST. We post the list of videos that passed by December 12, and every payout by December 17.

Every video teaches someone and inspires the next person to make one, and more videos grow the pot. We want videos that are educational and entertaining enough that people want to watch.

06, Credits and your channel
Your name goes on the work

Your name goes on it. Win or not.

Every piece we publish names who filmed it, and where. That is required, and we print it where you can see it. If you go out and get a stellar answer, your name goes on it.

Every eligible submission goes into the archive at wethem.xyz, indexed and searchable, with your name on it as the person who filmed it, and where. Win or not, the work stays up under your credit.

All videos will be up on YouTube and surfaced on our website. If you post yours on YouTube and link to it, we will use that to add to our site and funnel viewers to your channel.

We count every qualifying submission as a contribution to our One Question initiative.

Showing up now counts later

Showing up now counts later. This is how we build it.
