Channel 5 Carnival, Denver, Colorado, 2026

The Carnival, sixteen pieces.

Andrew Callaghan’s Carnival is part talent show, part town hall, part variety show. Performers sign up from the crowd and the audience decides who moves on by decibel level. We filmed it from the floor, from the balcony, and in the line outside. Everything plays here, nothing loads until you press it.
