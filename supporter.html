FAQ.Real questions. Real answers.

Why are you asking for support?

I am bootstrapping this. Support buys me the time to work on it fully and to grow the team. I am asking for the runway it takes to get We Them Media established as a platform and to release everything we have already filmed, which is years of it.

Can I contribute one time instead of monthly?

Yes. One-time gifts go through Fractured Atlas in USD, or you can send any token to wethemmedia.eth on Optimism. Monthly supporters get direct community access and a voice in what we investigate, so we lead with monthly, but one-time works.

I am interested but not ready to contribute yet. Can I follow along?

Join the Discord. We post updates, share research, and build community there. No financial commitment required. Join the Discord.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. We are a fiscally sponsored project of Fractured Atlas, a 501(c)(3), so anything given through them is deductible in the US.
