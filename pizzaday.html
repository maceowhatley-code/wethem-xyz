06, How we pick
The winner, announced

The winner was picked live. A few of us on the team, in conversation.

We did not run this through an algorithm and we did not put it to a public vote on social. We sat down together on stream, watched the strongest submissions back to back, and decided in conversation. The team said out loud why one piece was moving us and another was not, so the standard stayed visible.

What we asked of each piece. Does it show who was actually there? Do the people in it speak for themselves, in their own words, rather than being talked over? Does it bring in a range of voices? And do you still remember it an hour after you have scrolled past?
