One Question, a We Them Media programme

If you could ask people around the world one question, what would you ask them?

Then answer it yourself. Type it here, film yourself asking it, or put it to someone on your street. Every question we collect comes with its first answer, given by the person who asked it.

04
What happens to your answer

We read every one before anything goes up.

  • Nothing is shown until we approve it. If it goes up, we tell you where, by the email or handle you gave.
  • Your name goes on it, or none. Anonymous is fine; ones with a name and a place are more likely to be approved. If you filmed it, your name is on the byline.
  • We say no to hate or slurs, anyone’s personal information, selling, and political campaigning. Questions about politics are fine.

In one of the videos? Message us on Instagram (@we_them_media) or X (@WeThemMediaHQ) and we will get back to you within two weeks. The panel headed Something wrong here? at the foot of the page it is on works too. If you want it down, it comes down: you do not have to give a reason and nobody will ask you for one. That holds for anybody in a We Them Media video, whenever it was filmed and whoever filmed it.

05
Why one question

The same question, in as many places as we can reach.

Most reporting picks a place and asks whatever the story needs. One Question fixes the question first and puts it to people on their own streets, wherever the network reaches, so somebody in Lagos and somebody in Philadelphia end up holding the same question and can hear how the other answered it. Asking people to answer their own question is what makes it work: nobody poses a question they have not thought about. And every answer recruits the next asker, because the people who answer are asked to go and ask it themselves. It grew out of #QueryTheWorld, now the hashtag we file under.
