For creators, authors, shops and anyone ready to show up online with intent

The 90-Day Relaunch.

You have the stories. Your clients never see them. Start with a free conversation about what you already have.

We help your business run smoothly and help more people find you, with personable, value-first content that shows why you are the one to go to for what you do.

We do the rest for ninety days: we produce your socials from what you made, run an intentional relaunch of your accounts, build you a website if you do not have one, and help you start a newsletter. At the end you can run it yourself or keep us on at $500 a month.

Two ways to work with us: the Relaunch, for your content and your socials, and your business operating system, for the work behind them. Both start with a free conversation.

A financial adviser or an estate or elder-law attorney? How we work inside your compliance rules.

Book your free conversation Pick a 45-minute slot on Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday, between 10 am and 1 pm Eastern.

How it works

You record. We cut, caption and schedule it. Nothing posts without your OK.

  1. 1We look first. A free conversation about your work, your accounts and what you want people to do.
  2. 2You record, we build. We turn your recordings into your posts, a plan for the relaunch, your site and your newsletter.
  3. 3We run it with you. Ninety days of steady posting, a weekly check-in, and the numbers each month in plain words.

What you get

  • •Content coaching. What to record, how to say it, and what to leave out.
  • •Your socials, produced for ninety days. Short pieces cut from your recordings, captions, posting and replies.
  • •A relaunch with a point. One goal for each month and one next step in every post.
  • •Content from your events. When you go to an event in your field, we help you make content there with your peers. It shows your expertise and the bigger picture of your field.
  • •Your week, sorted. We list the jobs that eat your time and mark which ones an assistant could take on and which stay with you.
  • •A website if you do not have one. Simple, yours, and built around that next step.
  • •A newsletter started. The list, the first issue and the habit, so you own your audience.
  • •A Client spotlight. A clearly labelled spotlight in The We Them Weekly and in our content, our end cards included, where it fits.

Who it is for

Creators, authors, shops, artists and people with something to say or sell, who have the material and want it working for them. If a neighbourhood should know about you and does not yet, this is for you.

What it costs

We promise the work and the numbers. Not sales, not followers. We promise to work as hard as we can, and to be as intentional as possible, to help you get results.

The 90-Day Relaunch is $2,500, paid in three monthly payments: $1,000 to start, then $750 at the start of month two and $750 at the start of month three. You can cancel at the end of any month; what you have paid covers the months we worked.

Want to try it first? One Relaunch month is $1,000, then $500 a month, month to month.

After the ninety days, $500 a month on contract, for as long as we are worth keeping. We keep your socials active and take the boring parts. We turn your videos and voice notes into your newsletter, and you approve the final draft. We maintain your website and fill the gaps.

Ad spend, printing and shipping are yours and are not included unless we agree otherwise in writing.

To start, book a free conversation. Or tell us about yourself first.

Your business operating system

For businesses that already make money, sell high-ticket services and want real work off their hands. We set up a small set of assistants that draft, sort, schedule and follow up. They connect to the tools you already use, run on your accounts, and are yours to keep.

It starts with a consultation. We look at how your business runs, then come back with a proposal: what your system would do, how long it takes to set up and what it costs. Setups start at $2,500, then from $500 a month to keep it running, with a report each month on what it did.

  • •We start with your week. What you and your team do over and over, and what a system could carry.
  • •We build it on your accounts. You own it, and you can take our access back at any time.
  • •We write it down. Plain step-by-step guides, so it keeps working when people change.
  • •A person checks. Nothing goes out under your name, and no money moves, without a human seeing it first.

See if we are a fit. Book a free conversation, or choose "Your business operating system" in the form and tell us how your business runs.
